George of Sofia

Γεώργιος ο εκ Σόφιας

Martyr

Feast day March 26

Holy New Martyr George of Sofia, burned for the faith.

St. George was a young Christian of Sofia in Bulgaria who lived under Ottoman rule. Tradition relates that he was pressed to accept Islam and, refusing steadfastly, was burned to death in 1515.

He is numbered among the New Martyrs of Bulgaria, and his memory is kept on March 26.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, George of Sofia is commemorated on March 26. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on April 8 of the civil calendar.
