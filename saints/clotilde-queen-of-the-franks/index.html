Clotilde, Queen of the Franks

Κλοτίλδη

Holy Woman

Feast day June 3

Righteous Clotilde, queen of the Franks, who brought her husband Clovis to the Christian faith.

Saint Clotilde, a Burgundian princess, married Clovis, king of the Franks, and by her prayers and example led him to be baptised, an event of great consequence for the Christian history of Gaul. After his death she withdrew to Tours, where she lived in prayer and works of mercy near the tomb of Saint Martin until her repose in 545.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Clotilde, Queen of the Franks is commemorated on June 3. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on June 16 of the civil calendar.
