Abdas and Benjamin

Αυδάς και Βενιαμίν

Hierarch

Feast day March 31

Hieromartyr Abdas, Bishop of Persia, and Martyr Benjamin the Deacon.

St. Abdas was a bishop in Persia in the fifth century who was put to death by King Yazdegerd after he destroyed a fire temple of the Zoroastrians and refused to rebuild it. His deacon Benjamin was imprisoned, released on condition that he stop preaching, and, continuing to preach Christ, was tortured and killed.

The Church keeps their memory on March 31.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Abdas and Benjamin is commemorated on March 31. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on April 13 of the civil calendar.
