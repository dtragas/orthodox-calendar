Euphrosyne

Ευφροσύνη

Holy Woman

Feast day August 11

Venerable Euphrosyne of Alexandria.

St. Euphrosyne of Alexandria was the only daughter of a wealthy and devout father in the fifth century. Desiring the monastic life and fleeing an arranged marriage, she secretly left her home and, concealing her identity in a men's monastery, spent many years in prayer, fasting, and hidden struggle.

Near the end of her life she was revealed to her own father, who had long mourned her, and who himself then took up the monastic life in her cell. She is commemorated on August 11.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Euphrosyne is commemorated on August 11. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on August 24 of the civil calendar.
