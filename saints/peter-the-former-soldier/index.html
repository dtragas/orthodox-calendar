Peter the Former Soldier

Πέτρος ο από στρατιωτών

Venerable

Feast day October 9

Venerable Peter of Galatia, the soldier who became a hermit.

Born Leo in Galatia, he served in the imperial army under the iconoclast emperor Theophilos and was taken captive by the Arabs. Delivered from prison through prayer, he vowed himself to God and became a monk with the name Peter.

He lived as a hermit on Mount Olympus in Bithynia and later near Constantinople, reposing in peace in the ninth century.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Peter the Former Soldier is commemorated on October 9. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on October 22 of the civil calendar.
