Abercius of Hieropolis

Αβέρκιος

Hierarch

Feast day October 22

Bishop Abercius of Hieropolis, Equal-to-the-Apostles.

St. Abercius was Bishop of Hierapolis in Phrygia in the second century, called Equal-to-the-Apostles for his missionary zeal. He overthrew the idols of his city, brought multitudes to faith in Christ by his preaching and healings, and journeyed as far as Rome and the lands of the East, everywhere confirming the churches.

His famous epitaph, composed by the saint himself, speaks in veiled language of the Eucharist and the universal Church, and stands among the most precious monuments of early Christianity. He is commemorated on October 22.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Abercius of Hieropolis is commemorated on October 22. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on November 4 of the civil calendar.
