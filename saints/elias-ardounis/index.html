Elias Ardounis

Ηλίας ο Αρδούνης

Martyr

Feast day January 31

New Monk-Martyr Elias Ardounis of Kalamata.

St. Elias Ardounis was a barber of Kalamata who had embraced Islam and later repented, becoming a monk on Mount Athos. Returning to Kalamata, he publicly confessed Christ and was burned alive in 1686.

He is commemorated on January 31.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Elias Ardounis is commemorated on January 31. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on February 13 of the civil calendar.
