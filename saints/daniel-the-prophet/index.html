Daniel the Prophet

Δανιήλ ο Προφήτης

Prophet

Feast day December 17

Holy Prophet Daniel and the Three Holy Youths Ananias, Azarias, Misael.

The holy prophet Daniel was carried as a youth into the Babylonian captivity, where he served in the courts of kings without ever compromising his faith. God gave him understanding of dreams and visions of the kingdoms of the world and of the Son of Man; cast into the den of lions for praying to his God, he was preserved unharmed. With him the Church honors the Three Holy Youths, Ananias, Azarias, and Misael, who walked unburned in the fiery furnace rather than worship the golden idol.

Their deliverance is sung in the Church as a figure of the Resurrection and of the Virgin Birth. They are commemorated on December 17.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Daniel the Prophet is commemorated on December 17. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on December 30 of the civil calendar.
