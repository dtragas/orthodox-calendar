Ninian of Whithorn

Νινιανός

Hierarch

Feast day September 16

St. Ninian, bishop and apostle of the southern Picts.

St. Ninian was a Briton of the fourth and fifth centuries who, tradition relates, studied in Rome and was sent as bishop to his people. He built a stone church at Whithorn in Galloway, called Candida Casa, and from it preached Christ to the southern Picts.

He reposed in peace and is commemorated on September 16.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Ninian of Whithorn is commemorated on September 16. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on September 29 of the civil calendar.
