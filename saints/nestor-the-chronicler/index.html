Nestor the Chronicler

Νέστωρ ο Χρονογράφος

Venerable

Feast day October 27

Venerable Nestor the Chronicler of the Kiev Caves, author of the Primary Chronicle.

Nestor entered the Kiev Caves monastery in his youth under Saint Theodosios and was ordained hierodeacon. He wrote the lives of Saints Boris and Gleb and of Saint Theodosios, and is honored as the author of the Tale of Bygone Years, the earliest chronicle of the Rus.

He reposed around 1114, and his relics rest in the Near Caves.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Nestor the Chronicler is commemorated on October 27. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on November 9 of the civil calendar.
