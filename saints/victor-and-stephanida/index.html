Victor and Stephanida

Βίκτωρ

Martyr

Feast day November 11

Holy Martyrs Victor and Stephanida.

The holy martyr Victor was a Christian soldier who suffered for Christ in the second century, refusing to sacrifice to idols and enduring cruel tortures with unshaken faith. Stephanida, the young wife of another soldier, saw his endurance, confessed Christ openly, and was put to death with him.

The Church honors them together as witnesses whose courage under torment revealed the power of grace. They are commemorated on November 11.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Victor and Stephanida is commemorated on November 11. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on November 24 of the civil calendar.

Who celebrates a name day

People with these names keep their name day on this feast:

Viktor, Viktoras, Victor, Viktoria, Victoria
