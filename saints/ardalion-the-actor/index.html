Ardalion the Actor

Αρδαλίων ο Μίμος

Martyr

Feast day April 14

Holy Martyr Ardalion the Actor, who confessed Christ upon the stage.

Ardalion was a mime who, during a performance mocking the Christian martyrs, was suddenly moved by grace and declared before the theatre that he truly believed in Christ. The audience thought it part of the play until he repeated his confession in earnest.

He was burned alive in the persecution of the early fourth century.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Ardalion the Actor is commemorated on April 14. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on April 27 of the civil calendar.
