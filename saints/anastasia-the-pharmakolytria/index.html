Anastasia the Pharmakolytria

Αναστασία η Φαρμακολύτρια

Holy Woman

Feast day December 22

Great Martyr Anastasia the Deliverer from Potions.

The great martyr Anastasia was a Roman Christian of noble birth who suffered under the persecution of Diocletian at the turn of the fourth century. She devoted her wealth and freedom to visiting imprisoned Christians, tending their wounds and strengthening them for their contest, until she herself was seized and, after many trials, put to death.

She is called Pharmakolytria, Deliverer from Potions, for the faithful seek her protection against poisons, spells, and every harmful thing. She is commemorated on December 22.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Anastasia the Pharmakolytria is commemorated on December 22. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on January 4 of the civil calendar.
