Athanasios of Athos

Αθανάσιος Αθωνίτης

Venerable

Feast day July 5

Founder of the Great Lavra of Mount Athos.

St. Athanasios the Athonite was born in Trebizond in the tenth century. Drawn from a promising career in Constantinople to the monastic life, he came to Mount Athos and there founded the Great Lavra, the first of the great communal monasteries of the Holy Mountain, with the support of the Emperor Nikephoros Phokas.

Under his guidance Athos grew into the chief center of Orthodox monasticism, as it remains to this day. According to tradition he ended his earthly life during the collapse of a church structure under construction at the Lavra. He is commemorated on July 5.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Athanasios of Athos is commemorated on July 5. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on July 18 of the civil calendar.

Who celebrates a name day

People with these names keep their name day on this feast:

Athanasios, Thanassis, Thanos, Nasis, Athanasius
