Phanourios

Φανούριος

Martyr

Feast day August 27

Holy Great Martyr Phanourios the Newly Revealed of Rhodes.

St. Phanourios is a Great Martyr whose icon was discovered on the island of Rhodes in the fourteenth century, showing a young soldier surrounded by scenes of his sufferings for Christ. His name means the one who reveals, and nothing of his life is known beyond what the icon itself testifies: that he endured many tortures and gave his life rather than deny his Lord.

He is beloved throughout the Greek world as the revealer of lost things. According to tradition, the faithful bake a phanouropita, a simple spice cake, in his honor when asking his intercession, sharing it with others in remembrance of his mother.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Phanourios is commemorated on August 27. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on September 9 of the civil calendar.
