Ethelbert of Kent

Εθελβέρτος

Saint

Feast day February 24

St. Ethelbert, King of Kent, first Christian king of the English.

St. Ethelbert was King of Kent when St. Augustine and his monks arrived from Rome in 597. He received them kindly, was baptised, and allowed the free preaching of the Gospel, founding churches at Canterbury and elsewhere.

He reposed in 616 and is honoured as the first Christian king of the English.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Ethelbert of Kent is commemorated on February 24. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on March 9 (a day earlier in leap years) of the civil calendar.
