Founding of Constantinople

Εγκαίνια Κωνσταντινουπόλεως

Saint

Feast day May 11

Commemoration of the dedication of Constantinople as the New Rome by Saint Constantine the Great.

On May 11 in the year 330 the Emperor Constantine formally dedicated the city of Byzantium as the new capital of the empire, placing it under the protection of the Theotokos. The city became the seat of the Ecumenical Patriarchate and the heart of the Orthodox world.

The Church has kept the anniversary of the dedication each year.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Founding of Constantinople is commemorated on May 11. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on May 24 of the civil calendar.
