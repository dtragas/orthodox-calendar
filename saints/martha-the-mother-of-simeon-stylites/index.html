Martha the Mother of Simeon Stylites

Μάρθα

Holy Woman

Feast day September 1

Venerable Martha, mother of St. Simeon Stylites of the Wonderful Mountain.

St. Martha was the mother of St. Simeon the Stylite of the Wonderful Mountain, the younger stylite, and lived in the region of Antioch. A woman of great humility and constant prayer, she raised her son in piety and later lived near his pillar, serving the poor and the sick who came to him.

Tradition relates that she was warned of her death in a vision and reposed in peace; her son and his disciples honored her memory with a church. She is commemorated on September 1, alongside the great stylite Simeon the Elder.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Martha the Mother of Simeon Stylites is commemorated on September 1. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on September 14 of the civil calendar.
