Irene, Agape & Chionia

Ειρήνη

Martyr

Feast day April 16

Holy Martyrs Agape, Irene, and Chionia.

The holy virgins Agape, Irene, and Chionia were sisters from the region of Thessaloniki who suffered in the year 304, during the persecution of Diocletian. Arrested for their faith and for keeping the holy Scriptures when the emperor had ordered them destroyed, they refused to eat food sacrificed to idols or deny Christ, standing firm through interrogation.

Agape and Chionia were burned, and Irene, after further trials, followed her sisters in martyrdom. Three sisters crowned as one, they are commemorated on April 16.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Irene, Agape & Chionia is commemorated on April 16. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on April 29 of the civil calendar.
