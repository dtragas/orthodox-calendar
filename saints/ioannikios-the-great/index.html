Ioannikios the Great

Ιωαννίκιος ο Μέγας

Venerable

Feast day November 4

Venerable Joannicius the Great.

Venerable Ioannikios the Great was born in Bithynia in the eighth century and served for years as a soldier before leaving the world for the monastic life on Mount Olympus in Asia Minor. There he lived in great asceticism and prayer, receiving gifts of prophecy and wonderworking, and he stood with the venerators of the holy icons in the troubled years of iconoclasm.

He reposed in peace in old age, revered by monks and hierarchs alike as one of the great ascetics of his time. He is commemorated on November 4.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Ioannikios the Great is commemorated on November 4. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on November 17 of the civil calendar.
