The 150 Rhetors

Οι Εκατόν Πενήντα Ρήτορες

Saint

Feast day November 25

The 150 philosophers and rhetors converted by Saint Catherine and martyred by fire.

Tradition relates that the emperor Maxentius summoned the most learned philosophers of the empire to dispute with Saint Catherine. Defeated by her wisdom, they confessed Christ.

They were burned alive by order of the emperor and are commemorated with the Great Martyr.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, The 150 Rhetors is commemorated on November 25. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on December 8 of the civil calendar.
