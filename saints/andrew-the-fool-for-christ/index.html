Andrew the Fool-for-Christ

Ανδρέας ο διά Χριστόν Σαλός

Venerable

Feast day May 28

Venerable Andrew the Fool-for-Christ of Constantinople, who beheld the Protection of the Theotokos.

Andrew was a Scythian slave in Constantinople in the tenth century who, called by a vision, took up the life of folly for Christ, living in the streets in rags and enduring mockery while secretly praying through the night. He was granted many visions and the gift of insight.

With his disciple Epiphanios he saw the Theotokos spreading her veil over the people in the church at Blachernae, the vision commemorated at the feast of the Protection.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Andrew the Fool-for-Christ is commemorated on May 28. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on June 10 of the civil calendar.
