Kosmas and Damian (Asian)

Κοσμάς και Δαμιανός

Saint

Feast day November 1

Holy Unmercenary Physicians Cosmas and Damian of Asia.

Saints Kosmas and Damian of Asia Minor were brothers and physicians, raised in the faith by their pious mother Theodota. They treated the sick without ever accepting payment, asking only that those they healed believe in Christ, and for this the Church calls them Unmercenaries. They reposed in peace after lives given wholly to God and neighbor.

They are the first of several pairs of unmercenary physicians named Kosmas and Damian honored in the church year, and they are invoked by the sick and by those in the healing professions. They are commemorated on November 1.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Kosmas and Damian (Asian) is commemorated on November 1. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on November 14 of the civil calendar.

Who celebrates a name day

People with these names keep their name day on this feast:

Kosmas, Cosmas, Damianos, Damian, Argyris, Argiris, Anargyros, Argyro, Argiro, Anargyri, Damiani
