Synaxis of the Saints of Vatopedi

Σύναξις Βατοπαιδινών Αγίων

Saint

Feast day July 10

Synaxis of all the Saints of the Holy Monastery of Vatopedi on Mount Athos.

On this day the Monastery of Vatopedi on Mount Athos commemorates together all the saints who lived and labored within its walls: founders, ascetics, hierarchs and martyrs.

The synaxis gathers the many holy fathers of Vatopedi into one celebration.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Synaxis of the Saints of Vatopedi is commemorated on July 10. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on July 23 of the civil calendar.

Also commemorated on July 10

  • Anthony of the Kiev Caves

    Venerable, Αντώνιος

    Venerable Anthony of the Kiev Far Caves, founder of monasticism in Rus′ (†1073).

  • Forty-Five Holy Martyrs of Nicopolis

    Martyr, Μάρτυρες

    The Forty-Five Holy Martyrs of Nicopolis in Armenia, martyred under Licinius (c. 319).

  • Amalia

    Holy Woman, Αμαλία

    Name day for Amalia and Amelie in the Greek Orthodox tradition, commemorated with the Martyrs of Nicopolis.

    Name days: Amalia, Amalie, Amelie, Amelia, Amalberga

  • Bianor and Silouanos

    Martyr, Βιάνωρ και Σιλουανός

    Holy Martyrs Bianor and Silouanos of Pisidia.

  • Apollonios of Sardis

    Martyr, Απολλώνιος Σάρδεων

    Holy Martyr Apollonios of Sardis, crucified for Christ.

  • Ten Thousand Fathers of Nitria

    Martyr, Μύριοι Πατέρες

    The Holy Ten Thousand Fathers martyred in the desert of Nitria.

  • Parthenios and Eumenios of Koudoumas

    Venerable, Παρθένιος και Ευμένιος

    Venerable Parthenios and Eumenios, founders of the Koudoumas Monastery in Crete.
