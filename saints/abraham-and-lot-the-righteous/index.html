Abraham and Lot the Righteous

Αβραάμ και Λωτ

Saint

Feast day October 9

Righteous Forefather Abraham, friend of God, and his nephew Lot the Righteous.

Abraham was called by God to leave his homeland and go to the land of promise, and received the promise that in his seed all nations would be blessed. He is honored as the father of the faithful for his obedience, above all in his readiness to offer his son Isaac.

Lot, his nephew, lived righteously among the wicked of Sodom and was delivered by angels before the city's destruction.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Abraham and Lot the Righteous is commemorated on October 9. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on October 22 of the civil calendar.

Who celebrates a name day

People with these names keep their name day on this feast:

Avraam, Abraham, Abram, Avram, Lot
