Pancratius of Tauromenion

Παγκράτιος

Hierarch

Feast day July 9

Bishop Pancratios of Tauromenium.

St. Pancratius was, according to tradition, a disciple of the apostles who was sent to Sicily and became the first bishop of Tauromenium, present-day Taormina. There he preached the Gospel, overturned the worship of idols, and gathered a flock for Christ in the earliest days of the Church.

He completed his ministry as a martyr, put to death by enemies of the faith. He is honored as one of the apostolic founders of the Church in Sicily, and is commemorated on July 9.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Pancratius of Tauromenion is commemorated on July 9. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on July 22 of the civil calendar.
