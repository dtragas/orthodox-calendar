Trophimos the Apostle

Τρόφιμος

Apostle

Feast day September 19

Holy Apostle and Martyr Trophimus.

St. Trophimos was an early confessor of Christ who suffered in Asia Minor during the persecutions of the third century, together with his companions Sabbatios and Dorymedon. Refusing to take part in pagan festivity, they confessed the faith before their judges and endured torture and death for the name of Christ.

The three martyrs are honored together as faithful witnesses who preferred the eternal joy of Christ to the passing pleasures of the world. They are commemorated on September 19.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Trophimos the Apostle is commemorated on September 19. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on October 2 of the civil calendar.
