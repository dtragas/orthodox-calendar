Simeon of Persia and those with him

Συμεών Επίσκοπος Περσίδος

Hierarch

Feast day April 17

Hieromartyr Simeon, Bishop of Persia, and the many who suffered with him under Shapur.

Simeon bar Sabbae was bishop of Seleucia-Ctesiphon, the chief city of Persia, when King Shapur began his persecution of the Christians in the fourth century. Accused of loyalty to Rome and of refusing to worship the sun, he was arrested with many of his clergy.

Tradition relates that he was beheaded on Great Friday after watching a great multitude of his flock die before him, among them the priests Abdechalas and Ananias and the royal official Usthazanes.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Simeon of Persia and those with him is commemorated on April 17. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on April 30 of the civil calendar.
