Monica, Mother of Augustine

Μόνικα

Holy Woman

Feast day May 4

Saint Monica, whose years of prayer and tears brought her son Augustine to Christ.

Monica was born in North Africa in the fourth century to a Christian family and was given in marriage to a pagan husband, whom she eventually won to the faith by patience. She wept and prayed for many years for her gifted but wayward son Augustine.

She followed him to Milan, saw him baptized by Saint Ambrose, and died at Ostia in 387 while they were returning to Africa, saying she had nothing more to hope for in this life.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Monica, Mother of Augustine is commemorated on May 4. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on May 17 of the civil calendar.
