Barlaam the Venerable

Βαρλαάμ

Venerable

Feast day May 30

Venerable Barlaam, an ascetic who reposed in peace.

The synaxarion honors Venerable Barlaam on May 30, recording that he lived a life of asceticism and reposed in peace. He is distinct from the Martyr Barlaam of Antioch honored in November.

He is commemorated with the other saints of the day.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Barlaam the Venerable is commemorated on May 30. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on June 12 of the civil calendar.
