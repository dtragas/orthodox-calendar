Timothy of Symbola

Τιμόθεος

Venerable

Feast day February 21

Venerable Timothy of Symbola.

St. Timothy of Symbola was a monk who lived the ascetic life at the monastery of Symbola in Bithynia, in Asia Minor. From his youth he embraced obedience, fasting, and unceasing prayer, and the synaxaria remember him as a vessel of purity who received from God grace to heal the sick and drive away evil spirits.

Having lived to old age in holiness, he reposed in peace. He is commemorated on February 21.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Timothy of Symbola is commemorated on February 21. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on March 6 (a day earlier in leap years) of the civil calendar.
