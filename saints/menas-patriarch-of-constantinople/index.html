Menas Patriarch of Constantinople

Μηνάς Κωνσταντινουπόλεως

Hierarch

Feast day August 25

Saint Menas, Archbishop of Constantinople.

Saint Menas was Patriarch of Constantinople in the reign of Justinian the Great, in whose time the Church of Hagia Sophia was built. He presided over the Church in the years before the Fifth Ecumenical Council.

He reposed in peace in the sixth century.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Menas Patriarch of Constantinople is commemorated on August 25. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on September 7 of the civil calendar.
