Theopemptos and his Companions

Θεόπεμπτος και οι συν αυτώ

Martyr

Feast day February 7

Holy Martyr Theopemptos and those with him, who suffered by the sword.

St. Theopemptos and his companions confessed Christ before their persecutors and were beheaded. The Synaxaria preserve only the fact of their martyrdom.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Theopemptos and his Companions is commemorated on February 7. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on February 20 of the civil calendar.
