James the Apostle, Son of Zebedee

Ιάκωβος Ζεβεδαίου

Apostle

Feast day April 30

Holy Apostle James, son of Zebedee, brother of John the Theologian.

St. James, the son of Zebedee and elder brother of St. John the Theologian, was called by the Lord from his father's fishing boat to become one of the Twelve. With Peter and John he belonged to the innermost circle of the disciples, present at the Transfiguration and at the agony in Gethsemane.

As the Acts of the Apostles records, he was the first of the Twelve to drink the cup of martyrdom, beheaded in Jerusalem by Herod Agrippa. The Church honors him as the protomartyr among the apostles, and commemorates him on April 30.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, James the Apostle, Son of Zebedee is commemorated on April 30. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on May 13 of the civil calendar.
