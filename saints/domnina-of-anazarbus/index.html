Domnina of Anazarbus

Δομνίνη

Holy Woman

Feast day October 12

Holy Martyr Domnina of Anazarbus in Cilicia.

Domnina was a Christian of Anazarbus who was brought before the governor Lysias during the persecution of Diocletian. She confessed Christ and was scourged, her feet were burned, and she was cast into prison.

There she gave up her soul to God, receiving the crown of martyrdom at the end of the third century.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Domnina of Anazarbus is commemorated on October 12. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on October 25 of the civil calendar.
