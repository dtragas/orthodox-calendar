Mamas

Μάμας

Martyr

Feast day September 2

Holy Martyr Mamas of Caesarea.

St. Mamas was a young shepherd of Caesarea in Cappadocia, born to parents who had themselves suffered imprisonment for Christ. Living simply in the hills with his flock, he confessed the faith openly during the persecutions of the third century and endured torture and death rather than deny his Lord.

He has been venerated from ancient times as a protector of shepherds and their flocks, and churches in his honor spread throughout Cappadocia, Cyprus, and Greece. He is commemorated on September 2.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Mamas is commemorated on September 2. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on September 15 of the civil calendar.
