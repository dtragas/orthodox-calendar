Zacharias the Shoemaker and John

Ζαχαρίας ο Σκυτοτόμος και Ιωάννης

Saint

Feast day November 17

Righteous Zacharias the Shoemaker and John, who lived hidden lives of virtue in Constantinople.

Tradition relates that John, a pious man of Constantinople, prayed to be shown someone with whom he would share eternal life, and was directed to Zacharias, a humble shoemaker. He found that Zacharias and his wife lived in poverty, giving away most of their earnings and keeping a secret vow of chastity.

The two are honoured together as examples of hidden holiness among laypeople.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Zacharias the Shoemaker and John is commemorated on November 17. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on November 30 of the civil calendar.
