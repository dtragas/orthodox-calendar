John the Hermit and the 98 Fathers of Crete

Ιωάννης ο Ερημίτης

Venerable

Feast day October 7

Venerable John the Hermit and the ninety-eight God-bearing Fathers who lived in asceticism with him in Crete.

John came from Egypt with thirty-six companions and settled in Crete, where they were joined by others until they numbered ninety-nine. They lived in caves near Akrotiri, and John, their leader, was known for his continual prayer and gift of wonderworking.

Tradition relates that in his old age, crawling on all fours from weakness, he was mistaken for an animal and shot by a hunter, and that he forgave the man before reposing.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, John the Hermit and the 98 Fathers of Crete is commemorated on October 7. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on October 20 of the civil calendar.
