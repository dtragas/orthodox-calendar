Dionysius of Athos, Founder of Dionysiou

Διονύσιος ο Αθωνίτης

Venerable

Feast day June 25

Venerable Dionysius of Koritsa, founder of the monastery of the Holy Forerunner (Dionysiou) on Mount Athos.

Saint Dionysius was born near Kastoria in the fourteenth century and went to Mount Athos, where his brother Theodosius was a monk. Guided, tradition relates, by a light he saw on a rock above the sea, he founded there the monastery of the Holy Forerunner, later known by his name, with the support of the emperor Alexios III of Trebizond.

He reposed in Trebizond around 1388 while seeking help for his monastery.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Dionysius of Athos, Founder of Dionysiou is commemorated on June 25. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on July 8 of the civil calendar.
