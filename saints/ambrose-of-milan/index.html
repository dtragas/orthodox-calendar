Ambrose of Milan

Αμβρόσιος

Hierarch

Feast day December 7

Archbishop of Milan, one of the four great Latin Fathers.

St. Ambrose was Archbishop of Milan in the fourth century and one of the great Fathers of the Western Church. Governor of the region when a disputed episcopal election threatened the city with strife, he was acclaimed bishop by the people while still a catechumen; he gave his wealth to the poor, defended the Nicene faith against Arianism, and did not fear to call even emperors to repentance. His preaching helped bring Blessed Augustine to conversion.

A teacher and hymnographer whose writings nourished East and West alike, he reposed in peace. He is commemorated on December 7.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Ambrose of Milan is commemorated on December 7. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on December 20 of the civil calendar.
