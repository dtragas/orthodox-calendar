Therapon of Sardis

Θεράπων Σάρδεων

Hierarch

Feast day May 27

Hieromartyr Therapon, Bishop of Sardis, beheaded under Valerian.

Therapon was bishop of Sardis in Lydia and brought many pagans to Christ. During the persecution of Valerian he was arrested, dragged in chains through the cities of Asia and, tradition relates, bound to a dry stake which afterward put forth leaves.

He was beheaded around 259. His name-day is widely kept on this date.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Therapon of Sardis is commemorated on May 27. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on June 9 of the civil calendar.
