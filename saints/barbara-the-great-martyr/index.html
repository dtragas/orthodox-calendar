Barbara the Great Martyr

Βαρβάρα η Μεγαλομάρτυς

Martyr

Feast day December 4

Great Martyr Barbara of Heliopolis, patron against sudden death.

St. Barbara the Great Martyr lived in the late third century. According to tradition, she was the daughter of a wealthy pagan, Dioskoros, who confined her in a tower; coming to faith in Christ, she confessed Him openly, and after cruel tortures she was put to death by her own father, upon whom judgment swiftly fell.

She is invoked as a protector against sudden and unprepared death, and by long tradition is the patroness of those who work with fire and explosives, including miners and artillerymen. Her feast on December 4 is a beloved name day for Barbara and Varvara.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Barbara the Great Martyr is commemorated on December 4. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on December 17 of the civil calendar.

Who celebrates a name day

People with these names keep their name day on this feast:

Varvara, Varoula, Babs, Barb, Barbie, Barbara
