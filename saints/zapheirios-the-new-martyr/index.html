Zapheirios the New Martyr

Ζαφείριος

Martyr

Feast day June 11

New Martyr Zapheirios, who confessed Christ during the Turkish period.

Saint Zapheirios is commemorated in the Greek synaxaria as a New Martyr who gave his life for Christ during the years of Ottoman rule. Few details of his life have been preserved, but Greeks named Zafeiris and Zafeiria keep their name day on this feast.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Zapheirios the New Martyr is commemorated on June 11. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on June 24 of the civil calendar.

Who celebrates a name day

People with these names keep their name day on this feast:

Zafeiris, Zafeirios, Zafiris, Zafeiria, Zafeiro, Zafiro, Zafira
