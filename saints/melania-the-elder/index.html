Melania the Elder

Μελανία

Holy Woman

Feast day December 31

Venerable Melania the Elder of Rome.

St. Melania was a Roman noblewoman of one of the wealthiest families of the empire who, in the era of the great flowering of monasticism, renounced rank and riches for the sake of Christ. She gave her vast possessions to the poor and to the churches, freed those enslaved to her household, and devoted herself to asceticism, prayer, and the service of the monks and holy places of the East, ending her days in the Holy Land.

She is honored as a model of voluntary poverty embraced out of love for God. She is commemorated on December 31.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Melania the Elder is commemorated on December 31. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on January 13 of the civil calendar.
