Zenaida and Philonilla

Ζηναΐδα και Φιλονίλλα

Holy Woman

Feast day October 11

Holy Women Zenaida and Philonilla of Tarsus, physicians and kinswomen of the Apostle Paul.

Zenaida and Philonilla were sisters from Tarsus in Cilicia, related to the Apostle Paul, who studied medicine and used their art to serve the sick freely in the name of Christ.

They settled in a cave near Demetrias in Thessaly, healing the bodies and souls of those who came to them, and reposed in peace in the first century.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Zenaida and Philonilla is commemorated on October 11. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on October 24 of the civil calendar.

Who celebrates a name day

People with these names keep their name day on this feast:

Zenaida, Zinaida, Zina, Zenais, Philonilla
