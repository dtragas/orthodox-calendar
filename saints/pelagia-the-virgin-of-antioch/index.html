Pelagia the Virgin of Antioch

Πελαγία η Παρθένος

Holy Woman

Feast day October 8

Holy Virgin Martyr Pelagia of Antioch, who chose death rather than dishonor.

Pelagia was a fifteen-year-old Christian of Antioch during the persecution under Numerian. When soldiers came to seize her, she asked leave to change her garments and, praying, threw herself from the roof of the house to preserve her purity.

Saint John Chrysostom and Saint Ambrose both praised her as a martyr for her chastity.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Pelagia the Virgin of Antioch is commemorated on October 8. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on October 21 of the civil calendar.
