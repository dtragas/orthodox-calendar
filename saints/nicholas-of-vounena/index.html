Nicholas of Vounena

Νικόλαος ο εν Βουνένοις

Martyr

Feast day May 9

Holy Martyr Nicholas the New of Vounena in Thessaly, a soldier who became an ascetic and was slain by invaders.

Nicholas was an officer of the Byzantine army stationed in Thessaly in the tenth century. Withdrawing to the forests of Vounena near Larisa, he lived as an ascetic with a small band of companions.

When barbarian raiders overran the region, his companions were killed and Nicholas, refusing to renounce Christ, was pierced with a lance beneath an oak tree. His relics are venerated in Thessaly and he is invoked for healing.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Nicholas of Vounena is commemorated on May 9. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on May 22 of the civil calendar.
