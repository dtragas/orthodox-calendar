Archangel Michael of Panormitis

Αρχάγγελος Μιχαήλ ο Πανορμίτης

Saint

Feast day November 8

Synaxis of the Archangel Michael at the monastery of Panormitis on the island of Symi.

The monastery of the Archangel Michael Panormitis on Symi in the Dodecanese is one of the most famous places of pilgrimage in Greece. Its wonderworking icon of the Archangel draws pilgrims from across the islands and the seafaring communities.

On the feast of the Synaxis of the Archangels the monastery holds its great festival, and sailors traditionally bring offerings in thanksgiving for protection at sea.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Archangel Michael of Panormitis is commemorated on November 8. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on November 21 of the civil calendar.
