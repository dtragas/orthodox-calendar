Athanasios of Brest

Αθανάσιος Μπρεστ

Hierarch

Feast day July 20

Hieromartyr Athanasios, abbot of Brest, who suffered for Orthodoxy.

St. Athanasios was abbot of the monastery of Brest-Litovsk in the seventeenth century and a fearless defender of Orthodoxy against the Unia. Arrested on false charges, he was shot in 1648.

This day commemorates the uncovering of his relics; his main feast is September 5.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Athanasios of Brest is commemorated on July 20. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on August 2 of the civil calendar.
