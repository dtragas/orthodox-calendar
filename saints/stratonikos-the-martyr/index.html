Stratonikos the Martyr

Στρατόνικος

Martyr

Feast day January 13

Holy Martyr Stratonikos, who suffered with St. Hermylos at Singidunum.

St. Stratonikos was the jailer of St. Hermylos at Singidunum, present-day Belgrade, during the persecution of Licinius. Moved by the sufferings of the deacon, he confessed himself a Christian and was tortured with him; the two were drowned in the Danube.

He is commemorated on January 13.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Stratonikos the Martyr is commemorated on January 13. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on January 26 of the civil calendar.
