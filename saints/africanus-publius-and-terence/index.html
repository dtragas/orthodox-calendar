Africanus, Publius and Terence

Αφρικανός

Martyr

Feast day March 13

Holy Martyrs Africanus, Publius and Terence of Carthage.

Sts. Africanus, Publius and Terence were martyrs of Carthage in North Africa who suffered in the persecution of Decius in the third century. Refusing to sacrifice to idols, they were tortured and beheaded together with a number of companions.

The Church honors them on March 13.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Africanus, Publius and Terence is commemorated on March 13. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on March 26 of the civil calendar.
