Peter of Argos

Πέτρος Άργους

Hierarch

Feast day May 3

Bishop Peter of Argos.

St. Peter, Bishop of Argos, was a monk of deep humility who lived in the ninth and tenth centuries. Long refusing episcopal office, he was at last persuaded to shepherd the Church of Argos in the Peloponnese, where he became a father to his flock, feeding the hungry in time of famine, ransoming captives, and caring for orphans and the poor.

He was revered even in his lifetime as a wonderworker, and after his repose his relics became a source of healing. He remains a beloved saint of the Peloponnese, commemorated on May 3.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Peter of Argos is commemorated on May 3. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on May 16 of the civil calendar.
