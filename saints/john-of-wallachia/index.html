John of Wallachia

Ιωάννης ο Βλάχος

Martyr

Feast day May 12

New Martyr John of Wallachia, a Romanian youth strangled in Constantinople for refusing Islam.

John was born in Oltenia in Wallachia in the seventeenth century and was taken captive by the Turks as a young man. Brought to Constantinople, he was repeatedly pressured to accept Islam and to yield to his captor's immoral demands.

He refused steadfastly and was hanged in 1662. He is honored by the Romanian and Greek Churches.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, John of Wallachia is commemorated on May 12. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on May 25 of the civil calendar.
