Martha, Mary, Kyria, Valeria and Marcia, Virgin Martyrs

Μάρθα, Μαρία, Κυρία, Βαλερία και Μαρκία

Holy Woman

Feast day June 6

Holy Five Virgin Martyrs Martha, Mary, Kyria, Valeria and Marcia of Caesarea in Palestine.

These five virgins of Caesarea in Palestine confessed Christ before the governor during the persecutions and were put to death by the sword. The synaxaria honour them together as a company of virgin martyrs.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Martha, Mary, Kyria, Valeria and Marcia, Virgin Martyrs is commemorated on June 6. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on June 19 of the civil calendar.
