Adrian and Natalia

Αδριανός και Ναταλία

Martyr

Feast day August 26

Holy Martyrs Adrian and Natalia.

Sts. Adrian and Natalia were a married couple of Nicomedia in the time of the persecution of Maximian, at the beginning of the fourth century. Adrian, an imperial official, was so moved by the courage of imprisoned Christians that he confessed Christ and was enrolled among them; his young wife Natalia, secretly a Christian, strengthened him in prison and stood by him through his tortures and martyrdom.

Natalia afterward guarded his memory faithfully and reposed in peace, counted among the confessors. They are commemorated together on August 26 as an image of marriage sanctified by faith.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Adrian and Natalia is commemorated on August 26. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on September 8 of the civil calendar.
