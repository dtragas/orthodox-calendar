Timothy, Polios and Eutychios

Τιμόθεος, Πόλιος και Ευτύχιος

Martyr

Feast day May 21

Holy Martyrs Timothy, Polios and Eutychios, deacons of Africa.

Timothy, Polios and Eutychios were deacons in the Roman province of Mauretania who preached Christ and were put to death for their faith. Few details of their martyrdom have been preserved.

They are commemorated on May 21.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Timothy, Polios and Eutychios is commemorated on May 21. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on June 3 of the civil calendar.
