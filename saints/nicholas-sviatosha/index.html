Nicholas Sviatosha

Νικόλαος ο εν τω Σπηλαίω

Venerable

Feast day October 14

Venerable Nicholas Sviatosha, Prince of Chernigov, monk of the Kiev Caves.

Nicholas, great-grandson of Yaroslav the Wise and Prince of Lutsk, left his wife, children and principality in 1106 to become a monk of the Kiev Caves, the first Russian prince to do so.

He served humbly in the kitchen and as gatekeeper, planted the monastery garden, and founded a hospital. He reposed in 1143 and is venerated as a wonderworker.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Nicholas Sviatosha is commemorated on October 14. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on October 27 of the civil calendar.
