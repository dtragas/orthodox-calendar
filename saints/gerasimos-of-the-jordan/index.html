Gerasimos of the Jordan

Γεράσιμος

Venerable

Feast day March 4

Venerable Gerasimos of the Jordan who tamed a lion.

St. Gerasimos was a fifth-century ascetic who founded a great monastery in the desert by the River Jordan, guiding many monks in a strict rule of poverty, silence, and prayer, and was counted among the great elders of the Palestinian desert.

He is best remembered for the lion who, according to tradition, was healed by the saint of a thorn in its paw and thereafter served him tamely until his death — an image of paradise restored around a man of God. The Church commemorates him on March 4.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Gerasimos of the Jordan is commemorated on March 4. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on March 17 of the civil calendar.
