Zacchaeus and Alphaeus

Ζακχαίος ο Διάκονος και Αλφαίος

Martyr

Feast day November 18 and December 18

Holy Martyrs Zacchaeus the Deacon of Gadara and Alphaeus the Reader, at Caesarea in Palestine.

November 18

Saint Zacchaeus was a deacon of the church of Gadara and Alphaeus a reader of Caesarea. During the persecution of Diocletian they were arrested, Zacchaeus for his open confession and Alphaeus for exhorting Christians not to sacrifice.

After tortures both were beheaded at Caesarea in Palestine.

December 18

The holy martyrs Zacchaeus, a deacon of Gadara, and Alphaeus, a reader of the church of Caesarea, suffered at Caesarea in Palestine at the beginning of the persecution of Diocletian. Their martyrdom is recorded by Eusebius, who tells that they were tortured and beheaded together.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Zacchaeus and Alphaeus is commemorated on November 18. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on December 1 of the civil calendar.

On the New Calendar, Zacchaeus and Alphaeus is commemorated on December 18. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on December 31 of the civil calendar.
