Catherine the Great Martyr

Αικατερίνη η Μεγαλομάρτυς

Martyr

Feast day November 25

Great Martyr Catherine of Alexandria.

St. Catherine the Great Martyr was a noble and brilliantly educated maiden of Alexandria, martyred at the beginning of the fourth century. According to tradition, she confessed Christ before the emperor and bested in debate the philosophers gathered to refute her, leading many to the faith. After torture on the wheel, which was broken by grace, she was beheaded.

Her relics rest at the great monastery of Sinai that bears her name. She is honored as a patroness of learning and students, and her feast, kept on November 25 in some traditions and November 25 in others, is a cherished name day for Katerina.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Catherine the Great Martyr is commemorated on November 25. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on December 8 of the civil calendar.

Who celebrates a name day

People with these names keep their name day on this feast:

Aikaterini, Katerina, Katia, Katina, Kate, Cathy, Cat, Katherine, Kathryn, Catherine, Ekaterini, Kaiti, Katie, Kathy
