Theopemptos

Θεόπεμπτος

Martyr

Feast day January 5

Holy Martyr Theopemptos.

St. Theopemptos was a bishop who suffered martyrdom during the persecution of Diocletian at the beginning of the fourth century. Tradition relates that his steadfastness under torture so moved a pagan sorcerer named Theonas that he too confessed Christ, was baptized, and shared in the crown of martyrdom.

Together the bishop and the converted sorcerer bear witness that the courage of the martyrs was itself a preaching of the Gospel. They are commemorated on January 5, the eve of Theophany.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Theopemptos is commemorated on January 5. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on January 18 of the civil calendar.
