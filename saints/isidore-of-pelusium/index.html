Isidore of Pelusium

Ισίδωρος

Venerable

Feast day February 4

Monastic saint and prolific letter-writer.

St. Isidore of Pelusium was an ascetic of Egypt in the fifth century, who lived in monastic retirement near the city of Pelusium at the mouth of the Nile. A man of wide learning and deep devotion to the Holy Scriptures, he revered the teaching of St. John Chrysostom and followed his example of bold and clear instruction.

He is remembered above all for his letters, written by the thousands to people of every rank, expounding the Scriptures and correcting faults with wisdom and charity. He is commemorated on February 4.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Isidore of Pelusium is commemorated on February 4. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on February 17 of the civil calendar.
