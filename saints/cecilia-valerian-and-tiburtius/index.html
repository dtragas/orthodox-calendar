Cecilia, Valerian and Tiburtius

Κικιλία, Βαλεριανός και Τιβούρτιος

Holy Woman

Feast day November 22

Holy Virgin Martyr Cecilia of Rome with her husband Valerian, his brother Tiburtius and the martyr Maximus.

Saint Cecilia was a Roman noblewoman of the third century who had dedicated herself to Christ. Married against her will to the pagan Valerian, she persuaded him to respect her vow, and he and his brother Tiburtius were baptized and became zealous Christians.

The two brothers were beheaded, and Cecilia, after surviving an attempt to suffocate her in her bath, was struck with the sword and died confessing Christ. She is honoured in the West as patroness of music.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Cecilia, Valerian and Tiburtius is commemorated on November 22. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on December 5 of the civil calendar.

Who celebrates a name day

People with these names keep their name day on this feast:

Cecilia, Cecile, Cecily, Cecelia, Kikilia, Cecillia
