Pachomios the Great

Παχώμιος

Venerable

Feast day May 15

Founder of communal monasticism in Egypt.

St. Pachomios the Great was born in Egypt at the end of the third century. A pagan conscript soldier, he was won to Christ by the kindness of Christians to the troops, and after baptism took up the ascetic life in the desert. At Tabennisi by the Nile he founded the first great communities of monks living a common life under a rule, and thousands gathered under his guidance.

With St. Anthony he stands at the head of Egyptian monasticism, the father of the cenobitic way that spread through the whole Church. He reposed in the fourth century and is commemorated on May 15.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Pachomios the Great is commemorated on May 15. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on May 28 of the civil calendar.
