Zacharias the Prophet

Ζαχαρίας

Prophet

Feast day September 5

Prophet Zacharias and Elizabeth, parents of John the Baptist.

The holy Prophet Zacharias was a priest of the Temple in Jerusalem and the father of St. John the Baptist. The Archangel Gabriel announced to him at the altar of incense that his wife Elizabeth, barren and advanced in years, would bear a son; and at the child's birth Zacharias prophesied concerning the coming salvation of Israel.

Tradition holds that he was slain in the Temple for refusing to betray his son during Herod's slaughter of the infants. With righteous Elizabeth he is commemorated on September 5.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Zacharias the Prophet is commemorated on September 5. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on September 18 of the civil calendar.
