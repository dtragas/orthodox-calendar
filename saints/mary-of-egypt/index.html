Mary of Egypt

Μαρία η Αιγυπτία

Holy Woman

Feast day April 1

Venerable Mary of Egypt, great penitent saint.

St. Mary of Egypt is the great example of repentance. Living in Alexandria in the fifth and sixth centuries, she spent her youth in sin; traveling to Jerusalem, she was held back from entering the Church of the Holy Sepulchre by an unseen power, and, praying before the icon of the Theotokos, she was converted. Crossing the Jordan, she lived some forty-seven years alone in the desert in fasting and prayer.

Near the end of her life she was found by the elder Zosimas, who heard her story and gave her Holy Communion. She is commemorated on April 1 and on the fifth Sunday of Great Lent.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Mary of Egypt is commemorated on April 1. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on April 14 of the civil calendar.

Who celebrates a name day

People with these names keep their name day on this feast:

Maria, Mary, Maro, Marianna, Marika, Mariam, Marie

    Venerable, Ευθύμιος Σουζδαλίας
