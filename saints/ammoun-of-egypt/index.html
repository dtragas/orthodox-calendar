Ammoun of Egypt

Αμμούν ο Αιγύπτιος

Venerable

Feast day October 4

Venerable Ammoun of Egypt, founder of the monastic settlement of Nitria.

Ammoun was compelled by his relatives to marry, but persuaded his bride to live with him in chastity as brother and sister. After eighteen years they separated by agreement, and he withdrew to the desert of Nitria, where he became the father of a great monastic settlement.

He was a friend of Saint Anthony the Great, who tradition relates saw his soul carried to heaven by angels at the hour of his death in the mid-fourth century.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Ammoun of Egypt is commemorated on October 4. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on October 17 of the civil calendar.
