Athanasios of Attaleia

Αθανάσιος εξ Ατταλείας

Martyr

Feast day January 7

New Martyr Athanasios of Attaleia, martyred in Smyrna.

St. Athanasios was born in Attaleia in Asia Minor and lived in Smyrna. Tradition relates that after being accused of having spoken words of conversion to Islam, he refused to abandon the Christian faith and was beheaded in Smyrna in 1700.

He is commemorated on January 7.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Athanasios of Attaleia is commemorated on January 7. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on January 20 of the civil calendar.
