Synaxis of the Theotokos

Σύναξη Θεοτόκου

Great Feast

Feast day December 26

Synaxis of the Most Holy Theotokos — day after Nativity.

On the day after the Nativity of Christ, the Church gathers in synaxis to honor the Most Holy Theotokos, from whom the Saviour was born in the flesh. Having celebrated the birth of the Lord, the faithful turn with gratitude to His Mother, through whose obedience the Incarnation came to pass.

This is among the most ancient feasts of the Mother of God, and it follows a pattern kept throughout the church year, in which the day after a Great Feast honors the principal servant of the mystery. The synaxis proclaims Mary truly Theotokos, the Birth-giver of God.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Synaxis of the Theotokos is commemorated on December 26. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on January 8 of the civil calendar.

Who celebrates a name day

People with these names keep their name day on this feast:

Emmanuel, Emmanouil, Manolis, Manos, Manuel, Maria, Mary, Maro, Marianna, Panagiota, Emmanouela, Emmanuela, Manuela
