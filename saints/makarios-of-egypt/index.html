Makarios of Egypt

Μακάριος

Venerable

Feast day January 19

Desert father and abbot of the Egyptian monks.

St. Makarios of Egypt, called the Great, was one of the foremost of the desert fathers of the fourth century. Retiring to the desert of Scetis, he became the father of the monks who gathered there, renowned for his humility, his gentleness toward sinners, and his deep experience of prayer.

Many of his sayings are preserved among the words of the desert fathers, and spiritual homilies handed down under his name have nourished the Church for centuries. He reposed at a great age and is commemorated on January 19.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Makarios of Egypt is commemorated on January 19. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on February 1 of the civil calendar.
