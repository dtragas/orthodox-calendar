Marinos the Elder

Μαρίνος ο Γέρων

Martyr

Feast day October 18

Holy Martyr Marinos the Elder of Anazarbus in Cilicia.

Marinos was an old man of Anazarbus who confessed Christ before the governor Lysias in the persecution of Diocletian. Despite his age he endured beatings and tortures.

He was finally beheaded, receiving the crown of martyrdom at the end of the third century.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Marinos the Elder is commemorated on October 18. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on October 31 of the civil calendar.
