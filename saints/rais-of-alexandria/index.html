Rais of Alexandria

Ραΐς

Holy Woman

Feast day September 23

Holy Virgin Martyr Rais of Alexandria.

St. Rais was a young Christian woman of Alexandria who, seeing a company of Christians in chains being led to the ship that would carry them to trial, freely joined them and confessed Christ. She was beheaded with them in Egypt in the persecution of the early fourth century.

She is commemorated on September 23.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Rais of Alexandria is commemorated on September 23. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on October 6 of the civil calendar.
