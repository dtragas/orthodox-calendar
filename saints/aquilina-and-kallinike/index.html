Aquilina and Kallinike

Ακυλίνα και Καλλινίκη

Holy Woman

Feast day May 9

Holy Women Martyrs Aquilina and Kallinike.

The synaxarion honors the holy women Aquilina and Kallinike on May 9, recording that they suffered for Christ. The details of their martyrdom are not preserved.

They are commemorated together with the Prophet Isaiah and the Great Martyr Christopher.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Aquilina and Kallinike is commemorated on May 9. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on May 22 of the civil calendar.

Who celebrates a name day

People with these names keep their name day on this feast:

Akylina, Aquilina, Kalliniki, Callinice
