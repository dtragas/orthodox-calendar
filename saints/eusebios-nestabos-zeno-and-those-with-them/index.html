Eusebios, Nestabos, Zeno and those with them

Ευσέβιος, Νέσταβος και Ζήνων

Martyr

Feast day September 21

Holy Martyrs Eusebios, Nestabos and Zeno, brothers of Gaza, with Nestor and Bousiris.

The holy martyrs Eusebios, Nestabos and Zeno were brothers of Gaza who were seized by a pagan mob in the reign of Julian the Apostate and killed, with their kinsman Nestor. Bousiris, a Christian of the same city, confessed Christ and died of his torments.

They are commemorated on September 21.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Eusebios, Nestabos, Zeno and those with them is commemorated on September 21. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on October 4 of the civil calendar.
