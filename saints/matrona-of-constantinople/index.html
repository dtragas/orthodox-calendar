Matrona of Constantinople

Ματρώνα η Οσία

Holy Woman

Feast day November 9

Venerable Matrona of Perge, who lived disguised as a monk and later founded a convent in Constantinople.

Venerable Matrona was born at Perge in Pamphylia in the fifth century and was married in Constantinople. Longing for the monastic life, she left her husband and, disguised as a eunuch named Babylas, entered a men's monastery, where her identity was eventually discovered.

After years of wandering and ascetic struggle she founded a convent in Constantinople and guided many women in the spiritual life, reposing at a great age.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Matrona of Constantinople is commemorated on November 9. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on November 22 of the civil calendar.
