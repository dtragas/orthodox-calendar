Three New Martyrs of Vrachori

Νεομάρτυρες Βραχωρίου

Martyr

Feast day September 29

The three New Martyrs of Vrachori, the present Agrinio, martyred under the Ottomans.

The Church of Aetolia keeps the memory of three Christians of Vrachori, the present Agrinio, who were put to death by the Ottomans for refusing to deny Christ. Their names are not preserved in the Synaxarion.

They are commemorated on September 29.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Three New Martyrs of Vrachori is commemorated on September 29. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on October 12 of the civil calendar.
