Mnemonius of Amathus

Μνημόνιος

Hierarch

Feast day June 16

Saint Mnemonius, Bishop of Amathus in Cyprus, predecessor of Saint Tychon.

Saint Mnemonius was bishop of Amathus in Cyprus in the fifth century and ordained Saint Tychon, who succeeded him in that see. He is remembered as a holy hierarch who reposed in peace, and his memory is kept on the feast of his disciple.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Mnemonius of Amathus is commemorated on June 16. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on June 29 of the civil calendar.
