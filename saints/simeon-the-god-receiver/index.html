Simeon the God-Receiver

Συμεών

Venerable

Feast day February 3

The Elder Simeon who received the infant Christ in the Temple.

The righteous Simeon the God-Receiver was the elder to whom it was revealed by the Holy Spirit that he would not see death before he had seen the Christ of the Lord. When the infant Jesus was brought to the Temple forty days after His birth, Simeon received Him into his arms and blessed God with the prayer sung at every Vespers: Lord, now lettest Thou Thy servant depart in peace.

He is honored on the day after the feast of the Meeting of the Lord, together with the Prophetess Anna who also bore witness to the Child. His memory is kept on February 3.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Simeon the God-Receiver is commemorated on February 3. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on February 16 of the civil calendar.
