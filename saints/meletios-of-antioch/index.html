Meletios of Antioch

Μελέτιος

Hierarch

Feast day February 12

Archbishop of Antioch, champion of Orthodoxy.

St. Meletios was Archbishop of Antioch in the fourth century, a gentle and steadfast champion of the Nicene faith. For his defense of the divinity of Christ against the Arians he was driven from his see into exile more than once, yet he never ceased to shepherd his flock in patience and peace.

Restored at last, he presided at the opening of the Second Ecumenical Council in Constantinople in 381, and reposed in the Lord while the council was in session, mourned by the whole Church. He is commemorated on February 12.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Meletios of Antioch is commemorated on February 12. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on February 25 of the civil calendar.
