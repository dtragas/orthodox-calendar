John the Cave-dweller of Cyprus

Ιωάννης ο Σπηλαιώτης

Venerable

Feast day September 26

Venerable John the Cave-dweller of Sia in Cyprus.

St. John the Cave-dweller lived as a hermit in a cave near the village of Sia in Cyprus, in prayer and asceticism, and reposed in peace. A church at the site keeps his memory.

He is commemorated on September 26.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, John the Cave-dweller of Cyprus is commemorated on September 26. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on October 9 of the civil calendar.
