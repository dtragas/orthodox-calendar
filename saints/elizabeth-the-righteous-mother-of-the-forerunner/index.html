Elizabeth the Righteous, Mother of the Forerunner

Ελισάβετ

Holy Woman

Feast day September 5

Righteous Elizabeth, wife of the Prophet Zacharias and mother of St. John the Baptist.

The righteous Elizabeth was of the daughters of Aaron and the wife of the priest Zacharias; the Gospel of Luke calls them both righteous before God. Barren and advanced in years, she conceived St. John the Forerunner by the promise of the Archangel Gabriel, and when the Virgin Mary came to visit her she was filled with the Holy Spirit and greeted her as the Mother of her Lord.

Tradition relates that during the slaughter of the innocents she fled with her infant son into the wilderness, where a rock opened to shelter them, and that she reposed there in peace. She is commemorated with her husband on September 5, a major name day in Greece.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Elizabeth the Righteous, Mother of the Forerunner is commemorated on September 5. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on September 18 of the civil calendar.

Who celebrates a name day

People with these names keep their name day on this feast:

Elisavet, Elisabet, Elisabeth, Elizabeth, Eliza, Elisa, Liza, Lisa, Elsa, Beth, Betty, Elsie, Elissavet
