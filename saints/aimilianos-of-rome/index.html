Aimilianos of Rome

Αιμιλιανός ο Ρωμαίος

Martyr

Feast day March 7

Holy Martyr Aimilianos of Rome, with Jacob and Marianos.

St. Aimilianos was a Christian of Rome, remembered in the Greek synaxaria together with Sts. Jacob and Marianos. Tradition relates that they confessed Christ during the persecutions and were put to death for their faith.

The Church keeps their memory on March 7.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Aimilianos of Rome is commemorated on March 7. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on March 20 of the civil calendar.
