Martha, Mary and Lycarion

Μάρθα, Μαρία και Λυκαρίων

Holy Woman

Feast day February 8

Virgin Martyrs Martha and Mary, and their brother the monk-martyr Lycarion, in Egypt.

The sisters Martha and Mary devoted their lives to spreading the Christian faith among women. When a pagan governor passed by their house they cried out that they were Christians, and their young brother Lycarion, a monk, joined them in their confession.

The governor tried to spare them because of their youth, but they refused to deny Christ and all three were crucified and slain by the sword.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Martha, Mary and Lycarion is commemorated on February 8. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on February 21 of the civil calendar.
