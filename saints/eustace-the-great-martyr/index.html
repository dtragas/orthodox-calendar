Eustace the Great Martyr

Ευστάθιος ο Μεγαλομάρτυς

Martyr

Feast day September 20

Great Martyr Eustathios Placidas.

St. Eustathios, called Placidas before his baptism, was a Roman general of the second century. According to tradition he was converted while hunting, beholding a vision of the Cross between the antlers of a stag; he was baptized with his wife Theopiste and their two sons, endured the loss of all his possessions and family, and after years of trial was reunited with them.

When he refused to offer pagan sacrifice after a military victory, he and his family were martyred together. He is commemorated on September 20.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Eustace the Great Martyr is commemorated on September 20. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on October 3 of the civil calendar.

Who celebrates a name day

People with these names keep their name day on this feast:

Efstathios, Efstathis, Stathis, Eustace, Eustathius, Efstathia, Stathoula, Theopisti, Theopiste
