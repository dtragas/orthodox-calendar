Stachys the Apostle

Στάχυς

Apostle

Feast day October 31

Holy Apostle Stachys, first Bishop of Byzantium.

St. Stachys was one of the Seventy Apostles, greeted by name by the Apostle Paul in his epistle to the Romans. Tradition relates that he was ordained by the Apostle Andrew as the first Bishop of Byzantium, the city that would one day become Constantinople, where he shepherded the young Christian community and labored in the preaching of the Gospel.

He is honored as the first in the long line of hierarchs of the imperial city. Together with the apostles Apelles, Amplias, Urban, Narcissus, and Aristobulus of the Seventy, he is commemorated on October 31.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Stachys the Apostle is commemorated on October 31. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on November 13 of the civil calendar.
