Niketas the Confessor

Νικήτας ο Ομολογητής

Venerable

Feast day October 13

Venerable Niketas the Confessor and Patrician, who suffered for the holy icons.

Niketas was a nobleman of Paphlagonia and a kinsman of the empress Irene, who made him a patrician and governor of Sicily; he represented the empress at the Seventh Ecumenical Council. He later became a monk.

Under the iconoclast emperors he was imprisoned and exiled for his defense of the holy icons, and reposed in peace in 836 honored as a confessor.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Niketas the Confessor is commemorated on October 13. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on October 26 of the civil calendar.
