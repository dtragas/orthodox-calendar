Joachim and Anne

Ιωακείμ και Άννα

Saint

Feast day September 9

Holy and God-Bearing Ancestors Joachim and Anne, parents of the Theotokos.

Saints Joachim and Anne were the parents of the Most Holy Theotokos. Righteous and devout, they remained childless into old age, bearing reproach with patience and beseeching God with tears; in answer to their prayer they were granted a daughter, Mary, whom they dedicated to the Lord in His Temple.

The Church honors them as the God-bearing ancestors of Christ, and their names are invoked at the close of every Orthodox service. Their synaxis is kept on September 9, the day after the Nativity of the Theotokos.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Joachim and Anne is commemorated on September 9. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on September 22 of the civil calendar.
