Paul and Juliana

Παύλος

Martyr

Feast day March 4

Holy Martyrs Paul and his sister Juliana of Ptolemais, with Stratonikos and companions.

Sts. Paul and Juliana were brother and sister from Ptolemais in Phoenicia who suffered under the emperor Aurelian in the third century. Arrested for confessing Christ, they endured cruel tortures; tradition relates that several of their guards, among them Stratonikos, Kodratos and Akakios, were converted by their steadfastness and were martyred with them.

All were beheaded, and the Church keeps their memory on March 4.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Paul and Juliana is commemorated on March 4. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on March 17 of the civil calendar.
