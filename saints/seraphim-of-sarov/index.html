Seraphim of Sarov

Σεραφείμ

Venerable

Feast day January 2

Beloved Russian monastic saint, wonderworker of Sarov.

St. Seraphim of Sarov, one of the most beloved saints of Russia, lived from the mid-eighteenth century until 1833. Entering the monastery of Sarov as a young man, he spent long years in seclusion in the forest in fasting, silence, and unceasing prayer, including, according to tradition, a thousand days and nights of prayer upon a rock.

In his last years he opened his cell to all, greeting every visitor as his joy with the paschal greeting that Christ is risen. Pilgrims beyond number received his counsel and healing. He taught that the aim of the Christian life is the acquisition of the Holy Spirit. His memory is kept on January 2.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Seraphim of Sarov is commemorated on January 2. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on January 15 of the civil calendar.
