Seventy Martyrs of Scythopolis

Εβδομήντα Μάρτυρες οι εν Σκυθοπόλει

Martyr

Feast day June 28

Holy Seventy Martyrs who suffered for Christ at Scythopolis in Palestine.

The Seventy Martyrs of Scythopolis are remembered in the synaxaria as Christians who confessed Christ together during the persecutions and were put to death. The details of their contest have not been preserved.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Seventy Martyrs of Scythopolis is commemorated on June 28. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on July 11 of the civil calendar.
