Narses, Isaac and Sositheus

Ναρσής, Ισαάκ και Σωσίθεος

Martyr

Feast day December 9

Holy Martyrs Narses the Persian, Isaac and Sositheus.

The holy martyrs Narses, Isaac and Sositheus are remembered together on this day. Narses suffered in Persia for refusing to worship the sun; tradition records that all three confessed Christ and were perfected by the sword.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Narses, Isaac and Sositheus is commemorated on December 9. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on December 22 of the civil calendar.
