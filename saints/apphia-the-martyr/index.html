Apphia the Martyr

Απφία

Holy Woman

Feast day February 20

Holy Martyr Apphia, wife of the Apostle Philemon.

St. Apphia is greeted by the Apostle Paul in his Epistle to Philemon as a beloved fellow-Christian; tradition holds her to be the wife of Philemon, in whose house at Colossae the Church gathered.

She suffered martyrdom together with her husband when pagans broke into the church during a festival and stoned the faithful, in the first century.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Apphia the Martyr is commemorated on February 20. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on March 5 (a day earlier in leap years) of the civil calendar.
