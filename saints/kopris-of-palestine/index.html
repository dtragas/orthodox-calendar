Kopris of Palestine

Κόπρις

Venerable

Feast day September 24

Venerable Kopris of the monastery of St. Theodosios in Palestine.

St. Kopris was found as a newborn on a dung-heap, from which he took his name, during a raid on the monastery of St. Theodosios in Palestine, and was raised by the monks. He lived there in humility his whole life and reposed in peace in old age.

He is commemorated on September 24.

Feast day

On the New Calendar, Kopris of Palestine is commemorated on September 24. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the feast on October 7 of the civil calendar.
