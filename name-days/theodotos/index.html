When is Theodotos’s name day?

Theodotos can be celebrated on 4 feasts in the Orthodox calendar. People keep the feast of the saint they were named for, or the one their family has always kept.

The saints behind the name

  • Theodotos the Martyr

    January 1, Martyr, Θεόδοτος

    The Holy Martyr Theodotos is commemorated on January 1. The synaxaria record that he confessed Christ before the pagan authorities and was beheaded for the faith; the details of his time and place have not been preserved.

    Name days: Theodotos, Theodotus

  • Theodotos of Kyrenia

    March 2, Hierarch, Θεόδοτος

    St. Theodotos was Bishop of Kyrenia in Cyprus in the early fourth century. During the persecution of Licinius he was arrested and subjected to severe tortures for his confession of Christ, enduring them with such courage that many pagans who witnessed his sufferings came to faith.

    Name days: Theodotos, Theodotus

  • Theodotus of Ancyra

    May 18, Martyr, Θεόδοτος

    The Holy Martyr Theodotus of Ancyra suffered under the persecution of Diocletian at the beginning of the fourth century. An innkeeper by trade, he secretly aided the persecuted Christians of the city; when seven holy virgins of Ancyra were put to death for their faith, tradition tells that Theodotus recovered their bodies and gave them burial, though it was forbidden on pain of death. For this he was seized, tortured, and beheaded.

    Name days: Theodotos, Theodotus
