When is Titos’s name day?

Titos can be celebrated on 3 feasts in the Orthodox calendar. People keep the feast of the saint they were named for, or the one their family has always kept.

The saints behind the name

  • Titus the Wonderworker

    April 2, Venerable, Τίτος

    St. Titus the Wonderworker was a monk who from his youth gave himself to the ascetic life, excelling in obedience, humility, and love for the brethren. He served Christ in a monastic brotherhood, and for the purity of his life he received from God the grace of working wonders, from which he takes his title.

    Name days: Titus, Titos

  • Titos

    April 4, Apostle, Τίτος

    St. Titus was a disciple and companion of the Apostle Paul, who called him his true son in the common faith. A Greek by birth, he accompanied Paul on his apostolic journeys, was entrusted with delicate missions to the churches, and received the epistle that bears his name, in which Paul instructs him in the ordering of the Church.

    Name days: Titos, Tito, Tite, Titus

  • Titus the Apostle

    August 25, Apostle, Τίτος

    Saint Titus was a native of Crete and a disciple and companion of the Apostle Paul, who addressed to him one of the Pastoral Epistles. Paul left him in Crete to organize the Church there, and he became its first bishop.

    Name days: Titos, Titus

What to say on a name day

The Greek wish is Χρόνια Πολλά (Chronia Polla), “many years.” A call or a short message on the day is what people remember. See name day wishes you can send and what a name day is.
