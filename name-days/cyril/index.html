When is Cyril’s name day?

Cyril can be celebrated on 4 feasts in the Orthodox calendar. People keep the feast of the saint they were named for, or the one their family has always kept.

The saints behind the name

  • Cyril of Alexandria

    January 18, Hierarch, Κύριλλος Αλεξανδρείας

    St. Cyril was Archbishop of Alexandria in the fifth century. He presided at the Third Ecumenical Council at Ephesus in 431, which condemned Nestorius and confirmed that the Virgin Mary is truly Theotokos, the Mother of God.

    Name days: Kyrillos, Kyril, Kyrilos, Cyril

  • Cyril of Jerusalem

    March 18, Hierarch, Κύριλλος

    St. Cyril was Archbishop of Jerusalem in the fourth century, a gentle and learned shepherd of the Holy City. His famous Catechetical Lectures, delivered to those preparing for baptism, remain one of the treasures of the ancient Church, setting forth the faith and the holy mysteries with clarity and warmth.

    Name days: Kyrillos, Kyril, Kyrilos, Cyril

  • Cyril and Methodios, Enlighteners of the Slavs

    May 11, Apostle, Κύριλλος και Μεθόδιος

    Cyril (baptized Constantine) and his elder brother Methodios were born in Thessalonica in the ninth century. Sent by the Emperor and Patriarch Photios to Moravia, they devised an alphabet for the Slavonic language and translated the Scriptures and services, so that the Slavs might worship in their own tongue.

    Name days: Kyrillos, Kirillos, Cyril, Kyril, Methodios, Methodius
