When is Dorothea’s name day?

Dorothea can be celebrated on 2 feasts in the Orthodox calendar. People keep the feast of the saint they were named for, or the one their family has always kept.

The saints behind the name

  • Dorothea of Caesarea

    February 6, Holy Woman, Δωροθέα

    St. Dorothea was a devout maiden of Caesarea in Cappadocia who was arrested during the persecution of Diocletian. Two sisters who had lapsed from the faith were sent to sway her, but she brought them back to Christ and they were martyred with her.

    Name days: Dorothea, Dorothy, Dora

  • Theodora the Empress

    February 11, Holy Woman, Θεοδώρα η Βασίλισσα

    St. Theodora was the wife of the iconoclast Emperor Theophilos. Throughout his reign she secretly venerated the holy icons, and after his death, ruling as regent for her son Michael, she convened the council of 843 that restored the icons to the churches, an event celebrated each year as the Sunday of Orthodoxy.

    Name days: Theodora, Dora, Thea, Theodori, Dorothea

Also celebrating with Dorothea

These names share the feast, many of them other forms of the same name:

Dorothy, Dora, Theodora, Thea, Theodori

What to say on a name day

The Greek wish is Χρόνια Πολλά (Chronia Polla), “many years.” A call or a short message on the day is what people remember. See name day wishes you can send and what a name day is.
