When is Grigorios’s name day?

Most people named Grigorios keep their name day on January 25, the feast of Gregory the Theologian. The Orthodox calendar has 3 other feasts for the name.

The saints behind the name

  • Gregory the Theologian

    January 25, Great Feast, Γρηγόριος ο Θεολόγος

    St. Gregory the Theologian was born in Cappadocia in the fourth century and studied in Athens with his close friend St. Basil the Great. A man of deep prayer and unmatched eloquence, he was called to Constantinople when the city was dominated by Arianism, and through his celebrated theological orations restored the Orthodox faith in the Holy Trinity. He briefly served as Archbishop of Constantinople before withdrawing to a life of quiet and asceticism.

    Name days: Grigorios, Grigoris, Gregory, Greg, Grigoria

  • Gregory of Nyssa

    January 10, Hierarch, Γρηγόριος Νύσσης

    St. Gregory of Nyssa was the younger brother of St. Basil the Great, born in Cappadocia in the fourth century to a family rich in saints. As Bishop of Nyssa he defended the Nicene faith against Arianism, endured exile for the truth, and took part in the Second Ecumenical Council in Constantinople in 381.

    Name days: Grigorios, Grigoris, Gregory, Grzegorz

  • Gregory of Armenia

    September 30, Hierarch, Γρηγόριος Αρμενίας

    St. Gregory, the Illuminator of Armenia, brought the Gospel to the Armenian people at the turn of the fourth century. For his confession of Christ he endured long imprisonment in a deep pit, yet through his patience and prayer King Tiridates was converted, and Gregory, consecrated bishop, baptized the king and his people, making Armenia one of the first Christian nations.

    Name days: Grigorios, Gregory
