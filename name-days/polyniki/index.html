When is Polyniki’s name day?

Polyniki’s name day is September 1, the feast of 40 Holy Virgin Martyrs of Thrace.

Churches that follow the Old (Julian) Calendar keep the same feast on September 14 of the civil calendar. See everything commemorated on September 1.

The saint behind the name

  • 40 Holy Virgin Martyrs of Thrace

    September 1, Martyr, Αγίες Σαράντα Παρθενομάρτυρες

    The 40 Holy Virgins were ascetic women of Thrace who, with their teacher Ammon the Deacon, confessed Christ during the persecution under Licinius in the early fourth century. Refusing to sacrifice to idols, they endured imprisonment and torture with their teacher, and received together the crown of martyrdom at Heraclea in Thrace.
