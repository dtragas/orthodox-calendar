When is Gerasimos’s name day?

Gerasimos can be celebrated on 3 feasts in the Orthodox calendar. People keep the feast of the saint they were named for, or the one their family has always kept.

The saints behind the name

  • Gerasimos of the Jordan

    March 4, Venerable, Γεράσιμος

    St. Gerasimos was a fifth-century ascetic who founded a great monastery in the desert by the River Jordan, guiding many monks in a strict rule of poverty, silence, and prayer, and was counted among the great elders of the Palestinian desert.

    Name days: Gerasimos, Gerasim

  • Gerasimos of Cephalonia (Repose)

    August 16, Venerable, Γεράσιμος Κεφαλληνίας

    Saint Gerasimos was born in the Peloponnese in the sixteenth century, became a monk on Mount Athos and later settled on Cephalonia, where he founded a convent at Omala and served the people in famine and sickness. He reposed on the fifteenth of August 1579.

    Name days: Gerasimos, Makis, Gerasim, Gerasimoula

  • Gerasimos of Cephalonia (Translation of Relics)

    October 20, Venerable, Γεράσιμος ο εν Κεφαλληνία

    Gerasimos was born at Trikala in Corinthia in 1506 of the noble Notaras family. He became a monk on Mount Athos, lived in the Holy Land and on Zakynthos, and finally settled on Cephalonia, where he founded the convent of New Jerusalem at Omala.

    Name days: Gerasimos, Gerasimus, Makis, Gerasim, Gerasimoula

Also celebrating with Gerasimos

What to say on a name day

The Greek wish is Χρόνια Πολλά (Chronia Polla), “many years.” A call or a short message on the day is what people remember. See name day wishes you can send and what a name day is.
