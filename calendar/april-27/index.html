Orthodox saints and name days on April 27

The Orthodox Church commemorates 6 saints and feasts on April 27. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on May 10 of the civil calendar.

Saints and feasts of April 27

  • Simeon, Bishop of Jerusalem

    Martyr, Συμεών

    St. Simeon was a kinsman of the Lord according to the flesh, the son of Clopas, and became the second Bishop of Jerusalem after the death of James the Brother of God. He shepherded the mother Church of Jerusalem through decades of trial, including the destruction of the city, guarding the flock of Christ in the very places of His Passion and Resurrection.

    Name days: Simeon, Symeon

  • John of Kathares

    Venerable, Ιωάννης ο Ομολογητής

    John was abbot of the Monastery of Kathares in Bithynia in the ninth century. When the iconoclast emperors renewed their persecution he refused to renounce the holy icons and was imprisoned, scourged and exiled.

  • Poplion the Martyr

    Martyr, Ποπλίων

    Poplion is commemorated in the Greek synaxaria as a martyr who gave his life for Christ. Nothing further of his life has been preserved.

  • Eulogius the Hospitable

    Saint, Ευλόγιος ο Ξενοδόχος

    Eulogius was a stonecutter of Egypt who spent each day's wages in feeding and lodging the poor and travellers. Tradition relates that a holy elder prayed for him to receive wealth so that he might do more good, but riches led him astray until, at the elder's further prayer, he lost them and returned to his former humble life.

  • Lollion the New

    Martyr, Λολλίων ο Νέος

    Lollion is commemorated in the Greek synaxaria as a martyr who was put to death for Christ. The details of his life have not been preserved.

  • Stephen of Vladimir

    Hierarch, Στέφανος Επίσκοπος Βλαντιμίρ

    Stephen was a disciple of Saint Theodosius of the Kiev Caves and succeeded him as abbot of the monastery in the eleventh century, completing the great church he had begun. Later he founded a monastery of his own near Kiev.
