Orthodox saints and name days on May 21

The Orthodox Church commemorates 6 saints and feasts on May 21. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on June 3 of the civil calendar.

Saints and feasts of May 21

  • Constantine and Helen

    Great Feast, Κωνσταντίνος και Ελένη

    St. Constantine the Great, Equal-to-the-Apostles, was the first Roman Emperor to embrace Christianity. After his vision of the Cross and his victory under its sign, he ended the persecutions with the Edict of Milan, convened the First Ecumenical Council at Nicaea, and founded Constantinople as a Christian capital. His mother, St. Helen, traveled in old age to the Holy Land, where according to tradition she found the Precious Cross of the Lord and built churches on the holy sites.

    Name days: Konstantinos, Kostas, Kostis, Dinos, Konstandinos, Constantine, Costas, Costa, Gus, Konstantin, Eleni, Elena, Helen, Elli, Lena, Heleni, Konstantina, Constantina, Dina, Ntina, Kostoula, Helena, Ellen, Elenitsa, Nitsa, Lenio

  • Pachomios the New Martyr

    Martyr, Παχώμιος ο Νέος

    Pachomios was born in Little Russia, captured by the Tatars and sold to a Turk in Ousaki in Asia Minor, where he served for many years without abandoning his faith. Freed, he became a monk on Mount Athos under the elder Joseph.

    Name days: Pachomios, Pachomius

  • Kassianos the Greek

    Venerable, Κασσιανός ο Έλληνας

    Kassianos was a Greek nobleman named Constantine who came to Moscow in the entourage of Sophia Palaiologina. He became a monk at the Ferapontov Monastery and later founded a monastery near Uglich on the Volga.

    Name days: Kassianos, Cassian

  • Constantine of Murom with his sons Michael and Theodore

    Saint, Κωνσταντίνος του Μουρόμ

    Constantine was a prince of Kievan Rus who in the twelfth century was sent to rule Murom, then still largely pagan. His son Michael was killed by the pagans, and tradition relates that his patience and the wonders of an icon of the Theotokos won the people to baptism.

  • Christopher of Antioch

    Hierarch, Χριστόφορος Αντιοχείας

    Christopher was a Christian official who was chosen Patriarch of Antioch in the tenth century while the city was under Muslim rule. He was respected for his justice and his care for the Christians of the region.

  • Timothy, Polios and Eutychios

    Martyr, Τιμόθεος, Πόλιος και Ευτύχιος

    Timothy, Polios and Eutychios were deacons in the Roman province of Mauretania who preached Christ and were put to death for their faith. Few details of their martyrdom have been preserved.
