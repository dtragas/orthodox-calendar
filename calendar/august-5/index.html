Orthodox saints and name days on August 5

The Orthodox Church commemorates 6 saints and feasts on August 5. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on August 18 of the civil calendar.

Name days on August 5

Eusignios, Nonna, Nona

Saints and feasts of August 5

  • Eusignios

    Martyr, Ευσίγνιος

    St. Eusignios was a soldier of Antioch who served long years in the Roman armies, and according to tradition had served under Constantine the Great and beheld the appearing of the sign of the Cross. In extreme old age, in the reign of Julian the Apostate, he was denounced as a Christian; brought before the emperor, he boldly rebuked his apostasy and confessed Christ, for which he was beheaded.

    Name days: Eusignios

  • Nonna the Righteous

    Holy Woman, Νόννα

    Saint Nonna was the wife of Gregory of Nazianzus the Elder and the mother of Saint Gregory the Theologian, Saint Caesarius and Saint Gorgonia. Raised a Christian, she brought her husband to the faith and raised her children in piety.

    Name days: Nonna, Nona

  • Evgenios of Aitolia

    Venerable, Ευγένιος ο Αιτωλός

    Saint Evgenios Giannoulis was born in Aitolia in the seventeenth century and became a hieromonk and scholar. He founded schools in Agrafa and elsewhere, teaching the children of enslaved Greeks the faith and Greek letters.

  • Chrestos of Preveza

    Martyr, Χρήστος ο εκ Πρεβέζης

    Saint Chrestos was a young Greek from Preveza in Epirus who worked in Constantinople. Accused before the Ottoman authorities, he refused to deny Christ and was put to death in the eighteenth century.

  • Euthymios Patriarch of Constantinople

    Hierarch, Ευθύμιος Κωνσταντινουπόλεως

    Saint Euthymios was a monk and spiritual father to Emperor Leo the Wise before being raised to the patriarchal throne of Constantinople in the early tenth century. He was deposed and exiled amid the disputes over the emperor's fourth marriage.

  • John the New Chozebite

    Venerable, Ιωάννης ο Νέος Χοζεβίτης

    Saint John Jacob was a Romanian monk who left for the Holy Land and lived as a hermit in the Judean desert near the Monastery of Saint George of Choziba. He was known for strict asceticism, spiritual writings and gentleness toward all who sought him.
