Orthodox saints and name days on October 19

The Orthodox Church commemorates 6 saints and feasts on October 19. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on November 1 of the civil calendar.

Saints and feasts of October 19

  • Joel the Prophet

    Prophet, Ιωήλ

    The holy Prophet Joel was one of the twelve minor prophets, who prophesied in the kingdom of Judah. In a time of devastation he called the people to fasting and heartfelt repentance, and he foretold the day when God would pour out His Spirit upon all flesh.

    Name days: Ioel, Joel

  • Cleopatra the Righteous

    Holy Woman, Κλεοπάτρα

    Cleopatra was a widow of Edra near Mount Tabor whose husband had been a Roman officer in Egypt. Witnessing the martyrdom of Saint Varus, she secretly recovered his body and brought it home to Palestine, where she built a church in his honor.

    Name days: Cleopatra, Kleopatra

  • Varus and the Six Monk-Martyrs

    Martyr, Ούαρος

    Varus was a Roman officer in Egypt and a secret Christian who visited and cared for imprisoned confessors. When one of seven imprisoned monks died before trial, Varus took his place, declaring himself a Christian.

  • Sadoth of Persia

    Hierarch, Σαδώθ

    Sadoth succeeded Saint Symeon as Bishop of Seleucia-Ctesiphon in Persia during the persecution of King Shapur II. Arrested with his clergy and many of the faithful, he refused to worship the sun.

  • John of Rila

    Venerable, Ιωάννης της Ρίλας

    John was born in the village of Skrino near Sofia in the ninth century and became a monk while young. He lived for years in a hollow tree and then in a cave in the Rila mountains, in fasting and prayer, until disciples gathered and the great Rila monastery was founded.

  • Leontios the Philosopher

    Venerable, Λεόντιος ο φιλόσοφος

    Leontios is commemorated in the Synaxarion as a philosopher who forsook worldly wisdom for the ascetic life and reposed in peace. The details of his life are not preserved.
