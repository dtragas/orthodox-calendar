Orthodox saints and name days on May 10

The Orthodox Church commemorates 6 saints and feasts on May 10. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on May 23 of the civil calendar.

10May

Name days on May 10

Simon, Simeon, Alphaios, Alpheios, Philadelphos, Hesychios, Isychios, Hesychius, Erasmos, Erasmus, Lavrentios, Laurentius, Lawrence

Saints and feasts of May 10

  • Simon the Zealot

    Apostle, Σίμων ο Ζηλωτής

    The Holy Apostle Simon the Zealot was one of the Twelve chosen by the Lord. The Gospels record his name among the Apostles, and his title of Zealot bespeaks his burning devotion. After Pentecost he went forth like the others to preach the Gospel, and tradition holds that he carried the good news to distant lands and completed his course as a martyr.

    Name days: Simon, Simeon

  • Alphaeus, Philadelphus and Cyprinus

    Martyr, Αλφειός, Φιλάδελφος και Κυπρίνος

    Alphaeus, Philadelphus and Cyprinus were brothers, sons of a nobleman of southern Italy, who were arrested for their faith in the third century and taken through several cities before reaching Leontini in Sicily.

    Name days: Alphaios, Alpheios, Philadelphos

  • Hesychios of Antioch

    Martyr, Ησύχιος ο Ομολογητής

    Hesychios was a high-ranking official in Antioch in the reign of Maximian Galerius. When Christians were ordered to leave the imperial service, he willingly laid aside his rank, and when he refused to sacrifice he was thrown into the river Orontes with a millstone about his neck.

    Name days: Hesychios, Isychios, Hesychius

  • Erasmus and Onesimus with Fourteen Martyrs

    Martyr, Έρασμος και Ονήσιμος

    Erasmus and Onesimus, with fourteen companions, are remembered together with the brothers Alphaeus, Philadelphus and Cyprinus as martyrs of Sicily. They were converted through the witness of the three brothers and shared their confession and death.

    Name days: Erasmos, Erasmus

  • Laurence the Monk

    Venerable, Λαυρέντιος ο Όσιος

    The synaxarion records Venerable Laurence as a monk who lived a life of asceticism and reposed in peace. Some accounts place him in the monasteries near Constantinople.

    Name days: Lavrentios, Laurentius, Lawrence

  • Passarion, Agapios and Philemon

    Venerable, Πασσαρίων, Αγάπιος και Φιλήμων

    Passarion was a priest and archimandrite in Jerusalem in the fifth century, remembered for building a hospice for the poor and a monastery on Mount Sion. The monks Agapios and Philemon are honored with him.
