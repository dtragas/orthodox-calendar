Orthodox saints and name days on May 11

The Orthodox Church commemorates 6 saints and feasts on May 11. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on May 24 of the civil calendar.

11May

Name days on May 11

Mokios, Mocius, Kyrillos, Kirillos, Cyril, Kyril, Methodios, Methodius, Argyrios, Argyris, Argiris, Argyro, Olympia, Dioskoros

Saints and feasts of May 11

  • Mocius the Martyr

    Martyr, Μώκιος

    The Holy Hieromartyr Mocius was a priest who suffered for Christ at the beginning of the fourth century, during the persecution of Diocletian. Preaching boldly against the worship of idols, he was arrested and endured many torments, and was finally beheaded at Byzantium, faithful to the end.

    Name days: Mokios, Mocius

  • Cyril and Methodios, Enlighteners of the Slavs

    Apostle, Κύριλλος και Μεθόδιος

    Cyril (baptized Constantine) and his elder brother Methodios were born in Thessalonica in the ninth century. Sent by the Emperor and Patriarch Photios to Moravia, they devised an alphabet for the Slavonic language and translated the Scriptures and services, so that the Slavs might worship in their own tongue.

    Name days: Kyrillos, Kirillos, Cyril, Kyril, Methodios, Methodius

  • Founding of Constantinople

    Saint, Εγκαίνια Κωνσταντινουπόλεως

    On May 11 in the year 330 the Emperor Constantine formally dedicated the city of Byzantium as the new capital of the empire, placing it under the protection of the Theotokos. The city became the seat of the Ecumenical Patriarchate and the heart of the Orthodox world.

  • Argyrios of Epanomi

    Martyr, Αργύριος ο Επανομίτης

    Argyrios was born in Epanomi near Thessalonica in the late eighteenth century and worked as a tailor in the city. When a Christian who had apostatized was being taken to be judged, Argyrios openly urged him to return to Christ, and was himself arrested.

    Name days: Argyrios, Argyris, Argiris, Argyro

  • Olympia and Euphrosyne of Mytilene

    Holy Woman, Ολυμπία και Ευφροσύνη

    Olympia was born in Constantinople and became a nun on the island of Lesbos, where she was joined by Euphrosyne. Tradition relates that pirates raided their monastery in the thirteenth century and, when the nuns refused to renounce Christ, put Olympia to death with nails.

    Name days: Olympia

  • Dioskoros the New

    Martyr, Διόσκορος ο Νέος

    Dioskoros was a Christian who confessed his faith before the Turkish authorities and, refusing to convert, was put to death, tradition placing his martyrdom at Smyrna. Few details of his life are preserved.

    Name days: Dioskoros
