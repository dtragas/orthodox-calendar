Orthodox saints and name days on April 22

The Orthodox Church commemorates 6 saints and feasts on April 22. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on May 5 of the civil calendar.

22April

Name days on April 22

Theodoros, Theodoris, Ted, Theodore, Nathanael, Nathanail, Nathaniel, Nearchos, Nearchus, Nearhos

Saints and feasts of April 22

  • Theodore Sykeon

    Hierarch, Θεόδωρος

    St. Theodore of Sykeon was born in a village of Galatia in Asia Minor in the sixth century. Given to prayer and fasting from childhood, he became a monk of extraordinary asceticism, and his holiness drew crowds seeking healing and counsel; against his will he was made a bishop, though he later returned to his beloved monastic quiet.

    Name days: Theodoros, Theodoris, Ted, Theodore

  • Nathanael the Apostle

    Apostle, Ναθαναήλ ο Απόστολος

    Nathanael of Cana in Galilee was brought to Jesus by Philip and, hearing that the Lord had seen him under the fig tree, confessed Him as the Son of God. He was among the disciples to whom the risen Christ appeared at the Sea of Tiberias.

    Name days: Nathanael, Nathanail, Nathaniel

  • Nearchos the Martyr

    Martyr, Νέαρχος

    Nearchos was a soldier at Melitene in Armenia and the close friend of Polyeuctus, whom he brought to faith in Christ before Polyeuctus's own martyrdom. He later confessed the faith himself.

    Name days: Nearchos, Nearchus, Nearhos

  • Gaius of Rome

    Hierarch, Γάιος Πάπας Ρώμης

    Gaius was Bishop of Rome at the end of the third century, a Dalmatian by birth and, tradition relates, a kinsman of the emperor Diocletian. He guided the Church through the years before the great persecution.

  • Gregory Gravanos of Nisyros

    Martyr, Γρηγόριος Γραβανός

    Gregory Gravanos was a Christian of the island of Nisyros in the Dodecanese who, in the years of Ottoman rule, confessed Christ before the authorities and was put to death.

  • Platon of Banja Luka

    Hierarch, Πλάτων Ιερομάρτυς Μπάνια Λούκα

    Platon Jovanovic was a Serbian hierarch who became Bishop of Banja Luka in Bosnia. When the Ustasha regime came to power in 1941 he refused to abandon his flock.
