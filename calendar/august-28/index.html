Orthodox saints and name days on August 28

The Orthodox Church commemorates 6 saints and feasts on August 28. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on September 10 of the civil calendar.

Moses, Moysis

Saints and feasts of August 28

  • Moses the Ethiopian

    Venerable, Μωυσής ο Αιθίοπας

    St. Moses the Ethiopian was in his youth a slave and then the leader of a band of robbers in Egypt, feared for his strength and violence. Coming to himself, he repented and entered the monastic life in the desert of Sketis, where through long struggle with his former passions he attained deep humility and gentleness, and was found worthy of the priesthood.

    Name days: Moses, Moysis

  • Diomedes and Lawrence

    Martyr, Διομήδης και Λαυρέντιος

    The synaxaria record that Saints Diomedes and Lawrence confessed Christ and were put to death for their faith.

  • Hezekiah the King

    Saint, Εζεκίας ο Βασιλεύς

    Hezekiah was king of Judah in the days of the Prophet Isaiah. He cleansed the Temple, destroyed the idols and trusted in God when Jerusalem was besieged by the Assyrians; the Scriptures relate that his prayer was heard and fifteen years were added to his life.

  • Anna the Prophetess

    Holy Woman, Άννα η Προφήτις

    Saint Anna was an aged widow of the tribe of Asher who served God with fasting and prayer in the Temple. The Gospel of Luke relates that she was present when the infant Christ was brought there and spoke of Him to all who awaited the redemption of Jerusalem.

  • Thirty-Three Martyrs of Heraclea

    Saint, Τριάκοντα Τρεις Μάρτυρες Ηρακλείας

    The synaxaria record that thirty-three Christians of Heraclea confessed Christ and were burned for their faith.

  • Fathers of the Kiev Caves

    Saint, Όσιοι Πατέρες Σπηλαίων Κιέβου

    On this day the Church honours together the many ascetics whose relics rest in the Far Caves of the Kiev Caves Lavra, founded by Saint Theodosios in the eleventh century.
