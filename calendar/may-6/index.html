Orthodox saints and name days on May 6

The Orthodox Church commemorates 6 saints and feasts on May 6. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on May 19 of the civil calendar.

6May

Name days on May 6

Iov, Job, Serafeim, Seraphim, Serafim, Varvaros, Benedicta, Benedikti

Saints and feasts of May 6

  • Job the Long-Suffering

    Prophet, Ιώβ ο Πολύαθλος

    The Righteous Job lived in the land of Uz in the age of the patriarchs, a man blameless and upright who feared God and turned away from evil. Tested by the loss of his wealth, his children, and his health, he refused to curse God, saying that the Lord gave and the Lord has taken away, blessed be the name of the Lord.

    Name days: Iov, Job

  • Seraphim of Dombou

    Venerable, Σεραφείμ ο εν Δομπού

    Seraphim was born in Boeotia in the sixteenth century and became a monk in the region of Helicon. After years of solitude he founded the Monastery of the Transfiguration on Mount Dombou near Levadeia, gathering many disciples.

    Name days: Serafeim, Seraphim, Serafim

  • Sophia of Kleisoura

    Holy Woman, Σοφία της Κλεισούρας

    Sophia Chotokouridou was born in Pontus in 1883 and came to Greece with the exchange of populations. She settled at the Monastery of the Nativity of the Theotokos at Kleisoura in western Macedonia, where she lived for decades in the fireplace of the church, in poverty, cold and continual prayer.

  • Barbaros the Soldier

    Martyr, Βάρβαρος ο Στρατιώτης

    Barbaros was a soldier in the army of Julian the Apostate who, according to the synaxarion, prevailed in single combat and was then discovered to be a Christian. He refused to sacrifice to the gods and was tortured and beheaded, together with Bacchus, Callimachus and Dionysius, who confessed Christ on witnessing his steadfastness.

    Name days: Varvaros

  • Benedicta the Roman

    Holy Woman, Βενεδίκτη

    Benedicta was a nun of a Roman monastery in the time of Saint Benedict, remembered for her purity of life. Tradition relates that Saint Benedict foresaw her death and that she reposed in peace after a holy life.

    Name days: Benedicta, Benedikti

  • Job of Pochaev

    Venerable, Ιώβ του Ποτσάεφ

    Job was born in Galicia in the sixteenth century and became a monk in his youth. He was later abbot of the Lavra of Pochaev, where he strengthened the community, printed Orthodox books and defended the faith against the Unia.
