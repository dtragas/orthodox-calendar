Orthodox saints and name days on September 2

The Orthodox Church commemorates 6 saints and feasts on September 2. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on September 15 of the civil calendar.

Name days on September 2

Mamas, Mammas

Saints and feasts of September 2

  • Mamas

    Martyr, Μάμας

    St. Mamas was a young shepherd of Caesarea in Cappadocia, born to parents who had themselves suffered imprisonment for Christ. Living simply in the hills with his flock, he confessed the faith openly during the persecutions of the third century and endured torture and death rather than deny his Lord.

    Name days: Mamas, Mammas

  • John the Faster of Constantinople

    Hierarch, Ιωάννης ο Νηστευτής

    St. John IV, called the Faster, was a goldsmith of Constantinople who embraced the monastic life and rose, against his own wishes, to the patriarchal throne in 582. He was famed for his extreme fasting, his almsgiving, and his gift of healing, and he composed canons of penance that were long used in the Church.

  • Theodosius of the Kiev Caves

    Venerable, Θεοδόσιος των Σπηλαίων

    St. Theodosius was born near Kiev in the eleventh century and from youth longed for the monastic life, fleeing his mother's opposition to join St. Anthony in the caves above the Dnieper. He became abbot of the Kiev Caves and gave the brotherhood the cenobitic rule of the Studion monastery.

  • Diomedes, Julian, Philip and those with them

    Martyr, Διομήδης, Ιουλιανός, Φίλιππος

    The holy martyrs Diomedes, Julian, Philip, Eutychian, Hesychius, Leonides, Eutychius, Philadelphus, Melanippus and the holy woman Parthagape suffered together for Christ in the time of the pagan persecutions. Tradition relates that they were put to death by various torments, some by fire and some by the sword, refusing to the end to deny the faith.

  • Eleazar and Phinehas the Righteous

    Saint, Ελεάζαρ και Φινεές

    The righteous Eleazar was the son of Aaron and succeeded him as high priest of Israel, serving beside Joshua in the division of the Promised Land. His son Phinehas is praised in Scripture for his zeal for the Lord, by which the plague among the people was stayed, and God granted him a covenant of everlasting priesthood.

  • Kosmas the Hermit and Confessor

    Venerable, Κοσμάς ο Ερημίτης

    St. Kosmas the Hermit is listed in the Greek Synaxarion on September 2 as an ascetic and confessor of the faith. Tradition relates that he lived in solitude and prayer and suffered for his confession of Orthodoxy, reposing in peace.
