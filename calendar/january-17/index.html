Orthodox saints and name days on January 17

The Orthodox Church commemorates 6 saints and feasts on January 17. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on January 30 of the civil calendar.

Saints and feasts of January 17

  • Anthony the Great

    Venerable, Αντώνιος ο Μέγας

    St. Anthony the Great, the father of monasticism, was born in Egypt in the middle of the third century. Hearing in church the Gospel call to sell all, give to the poor, and follow Christ, he gave away his inheritance and withdrew into the desert, where he spent the rest of his long life in prayer, labor, and fierce spiritual struggle.

    Name days: Antonios, Antonis, Tony, Antonio, Anton, Antony, Anthony, Antonia, Tonia

  • George of Ioannina

    Martyr, Γεώργιος ο εξ Ιωαννίνων

    St. George was a poor man from a village of Epirus who served as a groom in Ioannina. Accused by Muslims of having abandoned Islam, though he had always been a Christian, he refused every inducement to convert and was hanged in Ioannina in 1838.

  • Anthony the New of Veria

    Venerable, Αντώνιος ο Νέος

    St. Anthony the New was born in Veria in Macedonia and lived as a hermit in a cave by the river Haliacmon, where tradition relates he spent many years in solitude and was granted the gift of miracles. His relics are venerated in Veria, of which he is the patron.

  • Theodosios the Great

    Saint, Θεοδόσιος ο Μέγας

    St. Theodosios the Great was Roman emperor from 379 to 395. He established the Nicene faith as the faith of the empire, convened the Second Ecumenical Council at Constantinople in 381, and did public penance before St. Ambrose of Milan after the massacre at Thessaloniki.

  • Achillas the Venerable

    Venerable, Αχιλλάς

    St. Achillas was one of the desert fathers of Scetis in Egypt in the fourth and fifth centuries, whose sayings are preserved among those of the elders. He was known for the severity of his fasting and for his discretion toward the brethren.

  • Anthony of Meteora

    Venerable, Αντώνιος ο Μετεωρίτης

    St. Anthony of Meteora, of the Kantakouzenos family according to tradition, was a monk of the Meteora in Thessaly in the fifteenth century, associated with the monastery of St. Stephen. He lived in asceticism on the rocks and reposed in peace.
