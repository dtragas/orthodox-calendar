Orthodox saints and name days on October 23

The Orthodox Church commemorates 6 saints and feasts on October 23. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on November 5 of the civil calendar.

23October

Name days on October 23

Saints and feasts of October 23

  • Ignatius, Patriarch of Constantinople

    Hierarch, Ιγνάτιος

    St. Ignatius was Patriarch of Constantinople in the ninth century. The son of an emperor, he was tonsured a monk in his youth after his father's fall from power, and rose by his virtue to the patriarchal throne, which he occupied twice amid the turbulence of imperial politics, suffering deposition and exile for his firmness.

    Name days: Ignatios, Ignatius

  • James the Brother of the Lord

    Apostle, Ιάκωβος ο Αδελφόθεος

    James was the son of Joseph the Betrothed and is called the Brother of the Lord. He was chosen by the apostles as the first Bishop of Jerusalem, presided at the Apostolic Council, and composed the Epistle that bears his name and the earliest form of the Divine Liturgy.

    Name days: Iakovos, Iakov, Jacob, James, Jakob, Jacovos

  • Makarios the Roman

    Venerable, Μακάριος ο Ρωμαίος

    Makarios, according to the account of three monks who found him, was a Roman who fled from his wedding feast to seek God and was led by an angel to a cave in the far wilderness of Mesopotamia.

  • Petronios the Venerable

    Venerable, Πετρώνιος

    Petronios was a disciple of Saint Pachomios the Great in Egypt and was appointed by him to lead the community after his death. Tradition relates that he reposed in peace only a short time after his teacher, in the fourth century.

  • Nikephoros the Venerable

    Venerable, Νικηφόρος

    Nikephoros is commemorated in the Synaxarion as a venerable father who lived in asceticism and reposed in peace. The details of his life are not preserved.

  • Two Children Martyrs

    Saint, Άγια Δύο Παιδιά

    The Synaxarion of this day commemorates two children who confessed Christ and were put to death for the faith. Their names are not preserved.
