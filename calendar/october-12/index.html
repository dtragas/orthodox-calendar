Orthodox saints and name days on October 12

The Orthodox Church commemorates 7 saints and feasts on October 12. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on October 25 of the civil calendar.

Saints and feasts of October 12

  • Probus and Tarachos

    Martyr, Πρόβος

    Saints Probus and Tarachos, together with Andronicus, were Christians of Cilicia who suffered during the persecution under Diocletian at the beginning of the fourth century. Differing in age and station, they were united in their confession; brought repeatedly before the judges, they endured prolonged tortures with astonishing endurance and were finally put to death for Christ.

    Name days: Provos

  • Symeon the New Theologian

    Venerable, Συμεών ο Νέος Θεολόγος

    Symeon was born in Paphlagonia around 949 and raised in Constantinople, where as a young man he became the disciple of Symeon the Pious of the Studion. He was made abbot of the monastery of Saint Mamas, which he restored in life and discipline.

  • Domnina of Anazarbus

    Holy Woman, Δομνίνη

    Domnina was a Christian of Anazarbus who was brought before the governor Lysias during the persecution of Diocletian. She confessed Christ and was scourged, her feet were burned, and she was cast into prison.

  • Anastasia the Virgin

    Holy Woman, Αναστασία η Παρθένος

    Anastasia was a Roman maiden who lived in a community of virgins and was denounced as a Christian in the persecution of Decius. She endured many torments, refusing to deny Christ.

  • Juventinus and Maximus

    Martyr, Ιουβεντίνος και Μάξιμος

    Juventinus and Maximus were bodyguards of the emperor Julian the Apostate at Antioch. When Julian ordered that the food and water of the city be sprinkled with blood from idol sacrifices, they openly lamented the profanation.

  • Jason of Damascus

    Hierarch, Ιάσων επίσκοπος Δαμασκού

    Jason is commemorated in the Synaxarion as a Bishop of Damascus who shepherded his flock in holiness and reposed in peace. The details of his life are not preserved.

  • Synaxis of the Saints of Athens

    Saint, Σύναξη Αθηναίων Αγίων

    On this day the Church of Athens gathers in one commemoration all the saints of the city, from Dionysios the Areopagite, Hierotheos and Damaris of apostolic times to Philothei and the New Martyrs of the Turkish yoke.
