Orthodox saints and name days on April 26

The Orthodox Church commemorates 6 saints and feasts on April 26. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on May 9 of the civil calendar.

26April

Name days on April 26

Vasilis, Basil, Glafyra, Glaphyra, Glafira, Tamara, Tamar

Saints and feasts of April 26

  • Basil of Amaseia

    Hierarch, Βασίλειος

    St. Basil was Bishop of Amaseia in Pontus in the early fourth century, in the last days of the persecutions. A fearless shepherd who protected those who fled to the Church for refuge, he drew upon himself the anger of the emperor Licinius, and was put to death for his faithfulness, his body cast into the sea.

    Name days: Vasilis, Basil

  • Glaphyra the Virgin

    Holy Woman, Γλαφυρά

    Glaphyra was a maidservant of the empress Constantia, wife of Licinius. When the emperor sought to seduce her, the empress helped her flee in disguise to Amaseia, where Bishop Basil received her and she gave money to build a church.

    Name days: Glafyra, Glaphyra, Glafira

  • Stephen of Perm

    Hierarch, Στέφανος Επίσκοπος Περμ

    Stephen was born in Ustyug in the fourteenth century and became a monk in Rostov, where he learned Greek and the language of the pagan Zyrians of the north. Going among them as a missionary, he created an alphabet for their tongue and translated the services.

  • Leo of Samos

    Hierarch, Λέων Επίσκοπος Σάμου

    Leo is commemorated in the Greek synaxaria as a holy bishop of the island of Samos who shepherded his flock in piety and reposed in peace. The details of his life have not been preserved.

  • Tamara the Queen

    Holy Woman, Ταμάρα η Βασίλισσα

    Tamara reigned over Georgia from 1184 to 1213, a time of peace, learning and church-building remembered as the golden age of the kingdom. She was renowned for her piety, her justice and her generosity to monasteries and the poor.

    Name days: Tamara, Tamar

  • George of Gergeri and the Twelve New Martyrs

    Saint, Γεώργιος ο Πρεσβύτερος και οι Δώδεκα Νεομάρτυρες Γέργερης

    George, a priest of the village of Gergeri in Crete, and twelve of his fellow villagers were put to death by the Turks for their faith during the years of Ottoman rule.
