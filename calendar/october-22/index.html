Orthodox saints and name days on October 22

The Orthodox Church commemorates 6 saints and feasts on October 22. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on November 4 of the civil calendar.

Saints and feasts of October 22

  • Abercius of Hieropolis

    Hierarch, Αβέρκιος

    St. Abercius was Bishop of Hierapolis in Phrygia in the second century, called Equal-to-the-Apostles for his missionary zeal. He overthrew the idols of his city, brought multitudes to faith in Christ by his preaching and healings, and journeyed as far as Rome and the lands of the East, everywhere confirming the churches.

    Name days: Averikios, Averki

  • Alexander of Adrianople

    Hierarch, Αλέξανδρος ο Επίσκοπος

    Alexander was a bishop in the region of Adrianople who converted many pagans by his preaching. Brought before the governor, he refused to sacrifice to idols and was tortured.

  • Lot of Egypt

    Venerable, Λωτ

    Abba Lot was a Desert Father of Egypt in the fifth century whose sayings are preserved in the Paradise of the Fathers. Best known is his exchange with Abba Joseph, who told him that if he wished, he could become all flame.

  • Rufus the Venerable

    Venerable, Ρούφος

    Rufus is commemorated in the Synaxarion as an ascetic who lived in the wilderness and reposed in peace. The details of his life are not preserved.

  • Gregory of Methone

    Hierarch, Γρηγόριος Μεθώνης

    Gregory was Bishop of Methone in the Peloponnese at the outbreak of the Greek Revolution of 1821 and blessed the struggle of his people. Tradition relates that he was seized by the forces of Ibrahim Pasha and killed for his faith and nation.

  • Zacharias the Martyr

    Martyr, Ζαχαρίας ο Μάρτυς

    Zacharias is commemorated in the Synaxarion of this day among the martyrs who confessed Christ unto death. The details of his sufferings are not preserved.
