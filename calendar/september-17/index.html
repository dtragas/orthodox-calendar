Orthodox saints and name days on September 17

The Orthodox Church commemorates 7 saints and feasts on September 17. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on September 30 of the civil calendar.

Saints and feasts of September 17

  • Sophia and Faith, Hope, Love

    Holy Woman, Σοφία, Πίστις, Ελπίς, Αγάπη

    St. Sophia was a pious widow of Rome in the second century who raised her three young daughters, Faith, Hope, and Love, in devotion to Christ. During the persecution under the Emperor Hadrian the three girls confessed the faith one after another and were put to death before their mother's eyes, and Sophia, having buried them, soon followed them in death from her grief.

    Name days: Sophia, Sofia, Sofi, Elpida, Agapi, Pistis, Faith, Hope, Love

  • Agathokleia the Martyr

    Holy Woman, Αγαθόκλεια

    St. Agathokleia was a Christian servant in the household of a pagan couple, Nicholas and Paulina. Tradition relates that her mistress tormented her for years to make her deny Christ, and at last, unable to break her, put her to death.

    Name days: Agathokleia, Agathoklia, Agathoclia

  • Theodoti of Nicaea

    Holy Woman, Θεοδότη

    St. Theodoti was a wealthy Christian woman of Cappadocia who confessed Christ at Nicaea in the third century. Tradition relates that she endured long imprisonment and many torments before she was beheaded.

    Name days: Theodoti, Theodote

  • Maximos, Theodotos and Asklepiodoti

    Martyr, Μάξιμος, Θεόδοτος και Ασκληπιοδότη

    The holy martyrs Maximos, Theodotos and Asklepiodoti were leading Christians of Adrianople in Thrace who suffered in the persecution of the early fourth century. Tradition relates that they were brought before the emperor Maximian himself, endured many torments, and were beheaded.

  • Lucia the Widow and Geminianus

    Holy Woman, Λουκία η χήρα και Γεμινιανός

    St. Lucia was a Roman widow of advanced years who confessed Christ during the persecution of Diocletian. Tradition relates that her own son denounced her, and that Geminianus, moved by her constancy, believed and was baptized and suffered with her.

  • Peleus, Nilus and the 156 Martyrs of Palestine

    Saint, Πηλέας, Νείλος και οι συν αυτοίς

    The bishops Peleus and Nilus, the priest Zeno and the noblemen Patermouthios and Elias led a great company of Egyptian and Palestinian Christians who, condemned to the mines of Palestine in the persecution of the early fourth century, were at last put to death by fire and the sword.

  • Herakleidios and Myron of Tamassos

    Hierarch, Ηρακλείδιος και Μύρων

    St. Herakleidios was a native of Cyprus who, according to tradition, guided the Apostles Paul and Barnabas across the island and was ordained by them bishop of Tamassos, the first in that see. He labored to bring the Cypriots to Christ, and his disciple Myron succeeded him as bishop.

    Name days: Herakleidios, Iraklidios, Heraclides, Iraklidis
