Orthodox saints and name days on March 27

The Orthodox Church commemorates 6 saints and feasts on March 27. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on April 9 of the civil calendar.

Name days on March 27

Matrona

Saints and feasts of March 27

  • Matrona of Thessaloniki

    Martyr, Ματρώνα

    St. Matrona was a servant in Thessaloniki in the early centuries of the Church, a Christian in the household of a pagan-hearted mistress who hated her faith. Slipping away in secret to pray with the Church, she was discovered, beaten, and confined, yet she would not abandon Christ, and she died of the cruel treatment she received, a true martyr.

    Name days: Matrona

  • Philetos and Lydia

    Martyr, Φιλητός

    St. Philetos was a senator of Rome who, with his wife Lydia and their sons Theoprepios and Makedon, suffered under the emperor Hadrian. Tradition relates that they were sent to Illyria, where the governor Amphilochios, converted by their steadfastness, was martyred with them, together with the jailer Kronides.

  • Kirykos of Apros

    Venerable, Κήρυκος ο εν Άπρω

    St. Kirykos is a venerable father of Apros in Thrace whose name the Greek synaxaria record for March 27 with the note that he reposed in peace. Nothing further of his life has been preserved.

  • Paul of Corinth

    Hierarch, Παύλος Κορίνθου

    St. Paul was Bishop of Corinth in the tenth century, the brother of St. Peter of Argos. Tradition relates that he was a man of learning and holiness who shepherded the Church of Corinth with wisdom and reposed in peace.

  • Hanani the Prophet

    Prophet, Ανανί

    The prophet Hanani appears in the Old Testament as a seer in the days of King Asa of Judah. Because he rebuked the king for relying on the king of Syria rather than on the Lord, he was put in prison, as the Second Book of Chronicles relates.

  • John and Barouchios

    Martyr, Ιωάννης και Βαρούχιος

    Sts. John and Barouchios are martyrs whose names the Greek synaxaria record together for March 27. Tradition relates that they confessed Christ and were put to death by the sword.
