Orthodox saints and name days on October 5

The Orthodox Church commemorates 6 saints and feasts on October 5. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on October 18 of the civil calendar.

5October

Name days on October 5

Charitine, Haritini, Charitini, Methodia, Ermogenis, Hermogenes, Ermogenes

Saints and feasts of October 5

  • Charitine

    Holy Woman, Χαριτίνη

    St. Charitina was a young Christian woman who suffered martyrdom during the persecutions at the beginning of the fourth century. An orphan raised by a pious guardian, she lived in prayer and purity, and when she was denounced as a Christian she confessed Christ boldly, enduring cruel tortures until she gave her soul to God.

    Name days: Charitine, Haritini, Charitini

  • Methodia of Kimolos

    Holy Woman, Μεθοδία

    Methodia was born on the island of Kimolos in 1865 and was married as a young woman. When her husband was lost at sea, she took the monastic habit and enclosed herself in a cell beside the church of the Panagia, living in fasting, vigil and prayer.

    Name days: Methodia

  • Hermogenes the Hieromartyr

    Hierarch, Ερμογένης

    Hermogenes is commemorated in the Synaxarion as a hieromartyr and wonderworker who confessed Christ and was put to death for the faith. Tradition relates that he ended his martyrdom by fire.

    Name days: Ermogenis, Hermogenes, Ermogenes

  • Mamelchtha of Persia

    Holy Woman, Μαμέλχθα

    Mamelchtha was a pagan priestess of Artemis in Persia whose sister was a Christian. Moved by a vision of an angel, she came to faith and was baptized by the local bishop.

  • Eudokimos of Vatopedi

    Venerable, Ευδόκιμος ο Βατοπεδινός

    Nothing is known of the earthly life of this monk of Vatopedi. In 1840, when the monastery's ossuary was being rearranged, his relics were found in a kneeling posture, fragrant and holding an icon of the Theotokos.

  • John Mavropous of Euchaita

    Hierarch, Ιωάννης ο Μαυρόπους

    John Mavropous was one of the most learned men of eleventh-century Constantinople, teacher of Michael Psellos and a counsellor of the emperor Constantine IX Monomachos. He was later consecrated Metropolitan of Euchaita in Pontus.
