Orthodox saints and name days on October 29

The Orthodox Church commemorates 7 saints and feasts on October 29. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on November 11 of the civil calendar.

29October

Name days on October 29

Tasos, Anastasios, Anastasius, Anastasia, Natasa, Natassa, Tasoula, Tasia, Sia, Anastasie, Melitini, Melitine

Saints and feasts of October 29

  • Anastasios the Younger

    Hierarch, Αναστάσιος

    St. Anastasios is remembered as a hierarch who served the Church of Constantinople, of whom the synaxaria preserve little beyond his faithful service and holy repose.

    Name days: Tasos, Anastasios, Anastasius

  • Anastasia the Roman

    Holy Woman, Αναστασία η Ρωμαία

    Anastasia was orphaned at the age of three and raised in a convent near Rome by the abbess Sophia. At twenty, during the persecution of Decius, she was denounced as a Christian and brought before the governor Probus.

    Name days: Anastasia, Natasa, Natassa, Tasoula, Tasia, Sia, Anastasie

  • Abramios the Recluse and Mary

    Venerable, Αβράμιος και Μαρία

    Abramios fled his wedding to become a hermit near Edessa, and was later sent by the bishop to convert a pagan village, which he won by his patience. His orphaned niece Mary lived beside him in asceticism from childhood.

  • Melitine the Martyr

    Holy Woman, Μελιτίνη

    Melitine was a Christian of Marcianopolis in Thrace in the reign of Antoninus. Brought to the temple, she overthrew the idols by her prayer and converted many, including the wife of the governor.

    Name days: Melitini, Melitine

  • Anna renamed Euphemianos

    Holy Woman, Άννα η μετονομασθείσα Ευφημιανός

    Anna was a noblewoman of Constantinople in the ninth century who, widowed, gave her goods to the poor and, disguised as a man, entered a monastery on Mount Olympus in Bithynia with the name Euphemianos.

  • Timothy of Esphigmenou

    Martyr, Τιμόθεος ο Εσφιγμενίτης

    Timothy, born Triantaphyllos in Thrace, had once accepted Islam under pressure with his wife. Repenting, he went to Mount Athos and became a monk at Esphigmenou, and after preparation by prayer and fasting he returned to Adrianople to confess Christ publicly.

  • Athanasios of Sparta in Attaleia

    Hierarch, Αθανάσιος ο εκ Σπάρτης Ατταλείας

    Athanasios was a priest from Sparta in the region of Attaleia in Asia Minor who was falsely accused by the Turks and pressed to accept Islam.
