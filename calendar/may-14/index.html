Orthodox saints and name days on May 14

The Orthodox Church commemorates 6 saints and feasts on May 14. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on May 27 of the civil calendar.

14May

Name days on May 14

Isidoros, Isidore, Therapon, Therapontas, Leontios, Leontius, Aristotelis, Aristotle, Leandros, Leander

Saints and feasts of May 14

  • Isidore of Chios

    Martyr, Ισίδωρος

    The Holy Martyr Isidore suffered on the island of Chios in the middle of the third century, during the persecution of Decius. A soldier serving in the imperial fleet, he was denounced as a Christian, and when he refused to sacrifice to the idols he was tortured and beheaded, remaining steadfast in his confession to the end.

    Name days: Isidoros, Isidore

  • Therapon of Cyprus

    Hierarch, Θεράπων Κύπρου

    Therapon was a bishop in Cyprus who suffered martyrdom for Christ; the details of his life are not preserved. His relics remained on the island until the ninth century, when, tradition relates, the saint appeared and directed that they be moved to Constantinople before an Arab raid.

    Name days: Therapon, Therapontas

  • Leontios of Jerusalem

    Hierarch, Λεόντιος Ιεροσολύμων

    Leontios was born in Stroumitsa in Macedonia and became a monk of the Monastery of Saint John on Patmos, later serving as its abbot. In the twelfth century he was chosen Patriarch of Jerusalem and journeyed to the Holy Land, where the Crusaders prevented him from taking possession of his see.

    Name days: Leontios, Leontius

  • Mark of Crete

    Martyr, Μάρκος ο Κρής

    Mark was a Cretan who had denied Christ in his youth and lived as a Muslim in Smyrna. Stricken with remorse, he confessed his return to Christ before the judge and refused every inducement to recant.

  • John the Bulgarian

    Martyr, Ιωάννης ο Βούλγαρος

    John was a young goldsmith from Bulgaria who worked in Constantinople. Falsely accused and pressured to accept Islam, he confessed Christ openly before the court.

  • Aristotle and Leander

    Martyr, Αριστοτέλης και Λέανδρος

    The synaxarion honors the martyrs Aristotle and Leander on May 14, recording that they gave their lives for Christ. The place and time of their martyrdom are not preserved.

    Name days: Aristotelis, Aristotle, Leandros, Leander
