Orthodox saints and name days on June 16

The Orthodox Church commemorates 5 saints and feasts on June 16. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on June 29 of the civil calendar.

Saints and feasts of June 16

  • Tychon of Amathus

    Hierarch, Τύχων

    St. Tychon was Bishop of Amathus in Cyprus in the fifth century. The son of a baker, he was known from youth for his generosity to the poor, and as bishop he labored with apostolic zeal to turn the remaining pagans of the island from their idols to Christ, shepherding his flock with gentleness and prayer.

    Name days: Tychon, Tihon

  • Mark of Apollonias

    Hierarch, Μάρκος Επίσκοπος Απολλωνιάδος

    Saint Mark was bishop of Apollonias and, tradition relates, was arrested for his confession of Christ, tortured and put to death, receiving the crown of martyrdom as a hierarch.

  • Mnemonius of Amathus

    Hierarch, Μνημόνιος

    Saint Mnemonius was bishop of Amathus in Cyprus in the fifth century and ordained Saint Tychon, who succeeded him in that see. He is remembered as a holy hierarch who reposed in peace, and his memory is kept on the feast of his disciple.

  • Forty Martyrs of Rome

    Martyr, Σαράντα Μάρτυρες οι Ρωμαίοι

    The Forty Martyrs of Rome are remembered in the synaxaria as Christians who confessed Christ during the persecutions and were put to death by the sword. The details of their contest have not been preserved.

  • Five Martyrs of Nicomedia

    Martyr, Πέντε Μάρτυρες οι εν Νικομηδεία

    These five Christians confessed Christ at Nicomedia during the persecutions and, tradition relates, were put to death by fire. The synaxaria honour them together on this day.
