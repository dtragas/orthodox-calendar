Orthodox saints and name days on November 10

The Orthodox Church commemorates 7 saints and feasts on November 10. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on November 23 of the civil calendar.

10November

Name days on November 10

Erastos, Arsenios, Arsenis, Arsenius, Orestis, Orestes

Saints and feasts of November 10

  • Erastos the Apostle

    Apostle, Έραστος

    The holy apostle Erastos, one of the Seventy, is named by the Apostle Paul in his epistle to the Romans as the treasurer of the city of Corinth. A companion of Paul in the work of the Gospel, he served the growing Church with the same faithfulness he had shown in civic office.

    Name days: Erastos

  • Olympas, Herodion, Sosipater and Quartus the Apostles

    Apostle, Ολυμπάς, Ηρωδίων, Σωσίπατρος και Κουάρτος

    The Apostles Olympas, Herodion, Sosipater, Quartus and Tertius are among the Seventy and are greeted by Saint Paul in his Epistle to the Romans. Tradition relates that Herodion became bishop of Neopatras, Sosipater of Iconium, Quartus of Beirut and Tertius of Iconium after Sosipater.

  • Arsenios of Cappadocia

    Venerable, Αρσένιος ο Καππαδόκης

    Saint Arsenios was born in Farasa of Cappadocia in the nineteenth century and served as priest and teacher of his village for some fifty years, known for his ascetic life and the many healings worked through his prayers. He baptized the infant who would become Saint Paisios the Athonite.

    Name days: Arsenios, Arsenis, Arsenius

  • Orestes the Physician

    Martyr, Ορέστης

    Saint Orestes was a physician of Tyana in Cappadocia during the persecution of Diocletian. Arrested for his faith, he refused to sacrifice to the idols and was subjected to severe tortures.

    Name days: Orestis, Orestes

  • Nonnus of Heliopolis

    Hierarch, Νόννος

    Saint Nonnus was a monk of Tabenna who was made bishop of Heliopolis, and later of Edessa, in the fifth century. He is remembered chiefly for his preaching at Antioch, which brought the famous actress Pelagia to repentance.

  • Theosterictus of Symbola

    Venerable, Θεοστήρικτος ο εν Συμβόλοις

    Venerable Theosterictus was abbot of the monastery of Symbola on the Bithynian Olympus in the ninth century. Tradition relates that he suffered for the veneration of the holy icons during the iconoclast persecution.

  • Milos the Wonderworker

    Hierarch, Μίλος ο Θαυματουργός

    Saint Milos was a bishop in Persia in the fourth century, known as a wonderworker. When his flock rejected his preaching he travelled to Jerusalem and Egypt, and on his return continued to labour for the Gospel.
