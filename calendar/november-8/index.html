Orthodox saints and name days on November 8

Saints and feasts of November 8

  • Synaxis of Archangels Michael and Gabriel

    Great Feast, Αρχάγγελοι Μιχαήλ και Γαβριήλ

    On November 8 the Church celebrates the Synaxis of the Archangels Michael and Gabriel together with all the Bodiless Powers of heaven. Michael, whose name asks Who is like God, is honored as the chief commander of the heavenly hosts who stood firm when the rebellious angels fell; Gabriel is the messenger who announced the Incarnation to the Theotokos.

    Name days: Michail, Michalis, Michales, Mike, Miguel, Michele, Mikes, Gabriel, Gavriil, Angelos, Angeli, Angelo, Michael, Aggelos, Stamatis, Stamatios, Taxiarchis, Raphael, Rafail, Michaela, Gabriella, Gavriella, Gavriela, Angeliki, Aggeliki, Angela, Angelina, Stamatia, Stamatina, Matina, Rafaela

  • Olga of Alaska

    Holy Woman, Όλγα της Αλάσκας

    Saint Olga Michael was a Yup'ik woman of Kwethluk, Alaska, wife of the village priest and a midwife. She lived a hidden life of prayer, generosity to the poor and quiet care for women and children in her community, and reposed in 1979.

  • Archangel Michael of Panormitis

    Saint, Αρχάγγελος Μιχαήλ ο Πανορμίτης

    The monastery of the Archangel Michael Panormitis on Symi in the Dodecanese is one of the most famous places of pilgrimage in Greece. Its wonderworking icon of the Archangel draws pilgrims from across the islands and the seafaring communities.

  • Archangel Michael of Mantamados

    Saint, Αρχάγγελος Μιχαήλ ο Ταξιάρχης Μανταμάδου

    The monastery of the Taxiarchs at Mantamados on Lesbos houses a celebrated relief icon of the Archangel Michael. Tradition relates that after a pirate raid on the monastery, a surviving monk shaped the icon from clay mixed with the blood of the slain brothers.
