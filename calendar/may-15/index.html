Orthodox saints and name days on May 15

The Orthodox Church commemorates 7 saints and feasts on May 15. Churches on the Old (Julian) Calendar keep this day on May 28 of the civil calendar.

15May

Name days on May 15

Pachomios, Achillios, Achilleas, Achilles, Achillefs, Achileas, Varvaros, Kali, Kale, Silvanos, Silvanus, Panigyrios

Saints and feasts of May 15

  • Pachomios the Great

    Venerable, Παχώμιος

    St. Pachomios the Great was born in Egypt at the end of the third century. A pagan conscript soldier, he was won to Christ by the kindness of Christians to the troops, and after baptism took up the ascetic life in the desert. At Tabennisi by the Nile he founded the first great communities of monks living a common life under a rule, and thousands gathered under his guidance.

    Name days: Pachomios

  • Achillios of Larisa

    Hierarch, Αχίλλιος Λαρίσης

    Achillios was born in Cappadocia and, after travels in the Holy Land and Rome, was chosen Bishop of Larisa in Thessaly in the fourth century. He took part in the Council of Nicaea in 325, where tradition relates that he confounded the Arians by a miracle.

    Name days: Achillios, Achilleas, Achilles, Achillefs, Achileas

  • Barbaros the Myrrhstreamer

    Venerable, Βάρβαρος ο Μυροβλύτης

    Barbaros was a North African who came to Greece with pirates and lived as a brigand in the region of Aetolia. Moved to repentance, tradition relates, he confessed to a priest and spent years in penance in the wilderness, walking on all fours as a beast.

    Name days: Varvaros

  • Andrew the Hermit

    Venerable, Ανδρέας ο Ερημίτης

    Andrew was born in Monodendri in Epirus and lived as a hermit in a cave near Kalana in the mountains of Aetolia, tradition placing him in the thirteenth century. He spent his life in prayer and hardship and was known for the wonders worked through him.

  • Kale the Righteous

    Holy Woman, Καλή

    The synaxarion honors Venerable Kale on May 15, recording only that she lived a life pleasing to God and reposed in peace.

    Name days: Kali, Kale

  • Silvanos of Tabennisi

    Venerable, Σιλβανός ο Ταβεννησιώτης

    Silvanos had been an actor before entering the monastery of Saint Pachomios at Tabennisi, and at first kept his old frivolity. Warned by the saint that he would be expelled, he was moved to such repentance that he wept continually for the rest of his life.

    Name days: Silvanos, Silvanus

  • Panegyrios of Cyprus

    Venerable, Πανηγύριος

    Panegyrios was an ascetic of Cyprus, remembered for his prayer and for the wonders worked through him, tradition placing him near the village of Malounta. Few details of his life are preserved.

    Name days: Panigyrios
