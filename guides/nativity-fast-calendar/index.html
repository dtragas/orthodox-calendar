The Nativity Fast Calendar for 2026, Day by Day

The Nativity Fast is the forty days of preparation for Christmas. On the New Calendar it runs from November 15 to December 24; on the Old Calendar from November 28 to January 6. Below is every day of the 2026 fast, on both calendars, ready to print for your family, your Sunday school or your parish bulletin.

These are the guidelines of the Greek Orthodox Archdiocese, the same rules the Orthodox Calendar app uses. Practice differs between churches and parishes: many Slavic and monastic communities keep the fast more strictly. Follow your priest's guidance, and talk to him about any rule for health, pregnancy, age or work.

How the Nativity Fast is kept

The fast is sometimes called St. Philip's Fast, because it begins the day after his feast on November 14. In the Greek practice it has two parts.

  • November 15 to December 17: fish, wine and olive oil are allowed, except on Wednesdays and Fridays, which are kept strictly. The Entry of the Theotokos on November 21 is a feast with fish whatever day it falls on.
  • December 18 to 23: the fast tightens. Weekdays are strict, and wine and oil are allowed on Saturday and Sunday.
  • December 24, the Eve of the Nativity: a strict fast day, usually until the evening service.

Meat, dairy and eggs are not eaten at any point in the fast. From December 25 to January 4 (the Twelve Days) all foods are allowed. On the Old Calendar the same church dates apply, 13 days later on the civil calendar, so the Nativity is kept on January 7. The full year is in the Orthodox fasting calendar.
