Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Orthodox Calendar app ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. The short version: we do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

None. Orthodox Calendar has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party data SDKs. We do not operate any server that receives your data.

2. Contacts

If you choose to enable the name-day feature, the app asks permission to read your device Contacts so it can match your friends and family to the saint of the day. This matching happens entirely on your device. Your contacts are never uploaded, stored on a server, shared, or sold. Any name-day assignments you make by hand are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. You can revoke Contacts access at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Contacts.

3. Purchases (Tip Jar)

If you choose to leave a tip, the transaction is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We never see or receive your payment information. Apple's handling of purchases is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

4. Sharing Greetings

When you send a "Χρόνια Πολλά" greeting, the app simply opens your chosen messaging app (such as WhatsApp or the iOS Share Sheet) with a pre-filled message. We are not involved in sending or storing that message.

5. Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children. It is suitable for all ages.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact
